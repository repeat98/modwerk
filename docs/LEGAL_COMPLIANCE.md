# German/EU compliance preparation

This is an implementation and operator review record for the Modwerk launch branch, checked on 4 October 2026. It is not a legal certification. No production deployment, provider contract acceptance, database migration or real-user deletion is authorized by this document. The owner supplied the public individual-operator identity/address and receiving contact in this chat. The Impressum uses those details without assuming a company, registration or VAT number.

Keep `REGISTRATION_OPEN=false` and `PRIVACY_READY=false` until the following evidence has been reviewed. The server requires **both** flags to be exactly `true` to accept registrations; sign-in, recovery, rights requests and existing members remain available. Changing flags is an operator action after review, never proof of compliance by itself.

1. Confirm the current frontend/API origins, receiving support mailbox and published German/English notices. Verify the operator is reachable at the disclosed address. Reassess DDG § 5 and state media-law requirements if commercial/editorial activity changes. Do not invent a register, VAT number, profession, DPO or consumer-dispute duty. Individual operation does not automatically exempt a public community from data-protection rules.
2. Inventory the **actual accounts/plans and contracts** for Cloudflare Worker/D1/R2/Pages as used, GitHub Pages, Resend and Gmail. Review roles individually: not every provider acts only as a processor. Obtain/save applicable Article 28 agreements, instructions, subprocessors and technical measures privately. A public provider DPA link is not evidence that the owner has accepted it. Do not accept legal terms on the owner's behalf without specific approval.
3. Record actual hosting/storage locations, access from outside the EEA, the applicable adequacy/certification or SCCs, and any needed transfer assessment/supplementary measures. Check current certifications; an EU location hint is not an EU-only guarantee. Provider documents linked in the notice describe possible safeguards; before launch, ensure the notice names the safeguards actually applicable to each transfer and supplies access to them.
4. Record exact provider security/mail/support log and recovery retention and the encrypted backup rotation period. Check Resend open/click tracking is disabled for the sending domain; the app sends transactional emails with matching plain-text and inline HTML versions but domain settings must also be checked. Check that request bodies, passwords, links, IP addresses and recipient addresses are not logged by deployed observability. Set restricted access, MFA, least-privilege tokens and an encrypted operator device. Update notice retention with the verified periods; do not leave provider/archive periods unresolved at launch.
5. Assign the receiving-mailbox operator, daily rights/illegal-content queue review, incident escalation and backup owner. Test mailbox receipt and a fictional unlawful-content notice end to end. The report page prepares email; the application does **not** send an acknowledgement or decision automatically. A monitored mailbox, human acknowledgement and decision procedure are essential before accepting public user content.
6. Have German counsel assess applicability of the DSA (including whether the service is a hosting service/online platform), micro/small-enterprise exceptions, DDG/state media law, minors, and any future donations/commercial services. Review whether a DPO, DPIA or additional obligations are triggered. Small size does not waive all hosting obligations. No trade, marketplace, paid contract or newsletter flows have been added by this PR.
7. Apply migration 0018 with the reviewed account migrations, run synthetic API/mail checks, then authorize launch. Existing accounts are not retroactively recorded as agreeing to new rules. Communicate material rules changes personally before they apply; keep minimal evidence of the communication. The launch prerequisite is the reviewed verified-account/forum branch; do not enable legacy guest posting.

## Rights handling

- The verified member can obtain a consistently scoped JSON export after current-password verification. It contains own account metadata and activity, including hidden own posts and private issue logs, without password hashes, tokens, private operator notes, another member's text or firmware. It is a useful download, **not the entire Article 15 response**: review any further request for personal data in moderation notes, provider/support records and recipients, redact third-party data where appropriate, and provide the required processing information. Suspended users and anyone unable to sign in can use the public contact address; do not make website authentication the only way to exercise rights.
- The private removal queue retains its existing separately reviewed deletion procedure. Submitting a request does not erase data. New responses show the initial one-calendar-month deadline; administrator queues prioritize active/oldest requests. A status update is not the substantive GDPR response.
- Record every request (including mail) privately on receipt. Answer without undue delay and ordinarily within one month. If complexity/number permits an extension of up to two additional months, communicate the reason **within the original month** and record the actual notification. Calendar deadlines shown by the app clamp to shorter months; verify applicable public-holiday/weekend rules manually. A request for scope clarification does not automatically restart the deadline.
- Assess erasure grounds/exceptions, correction, restriction, objections and portability individually. Do not require additional consent or broad photo-ID collection for ordinary requests. Explain a refusal and complaint/judicial remedies. Inform relevant recipients of rectification/erasure/restriction where required. Remove `account_policy_acceptances`, `push_subscriptions` and `signup_events` rows in the approved account-removal transaction; completion checks require them absent. Removing a device subscription also removes its queued push deliveries. The `users` projection and public free text/attribution still need an explicit assessment, not merely changing a display name.
- Expire/remove provider/mail/archive copies under verified periods; keep restricted recovery copies out of ordinary use and reapply the minimal deletion ledger after any restore before reopening. Do not record unnecessary deleted-user details or publish requests/logs in GitHub.

## Notice and action / community rules

The Impressum publishes the individual operator, service address, receiving email and German/English human contact for users/authorities. Every layout keeps Privacy, Impressum and Report content reachable. The report page is accessible without authentication and explains exact URL/ID, reasons, contact and good-faith declaration, including the narrow identity exception for Directive 2011/93/EU Articles 3–7. It must never invite illegal material as an attachment.

The operator acknowledges received notices when contact details exist, checks specificity, investigates without undue delay and informs the notifier of the decision/redress. When imposing content/account restrictions, communicate the factual/legal/rules basis, scope/duration, automation involvement and available review to affected people where applicable; protect the notifier. Human moderation must consider context and fundamental rights. Preserve a minimal private decision record and offer personal review at the contact address. Urgent suspected criminal threats to life/safety may require authority notification under DSA Art. 18; counsel should prepare the route. Existing public profile visibility must not disclose private reports or reporter email.

Determine any DSA transparency-reporting, contact-form, complaint-system and platform-specific duties from the **actual service classification and applicable exemption**, rather than claiming this mail channel alone satisfies the whole DSA. Do not add a defunct EU ODR-platform link or unreviewed waiver of consumer rights.

## Incident response

Restrict affected access, preserve minimal private evidence, assess data/people/risks, and document decisions. GDPR Art. 33 can require notifying the competent authority within 72 hours of becoming aware unless the breach is unlikely to risk rights/freedoms; incomplete facts do not justify waiting beyond that period. Art. 34 may require informing affected people without undue delay where high risk applies. Keep a record even when reporting is not required. Use the LfDI Baden-Württemberg's actual reporting route. The existing [community runbook](COMMUNITY_OPERATIONS.md) covers secret/session rotation and recovery; recovery must preserve approved removals.
