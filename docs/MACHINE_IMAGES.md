Analog Four MKI has no suitable free photo yet (Commons only has teardown shots), so it shows the line drawing.

Zoom MS-70CDR, MS-50G, MS-60B and the G1on family use original SVG illustrations in `src/devices/ZoomPedalArt.tsx`, shared through `DeviceImage` across cards, machine pages and the switcher. The blue, silver and red MultiStomp enclosures and the charcoal G1on with blue controls and an amber display distinguish the models. These are authored vectors with illustrative LCD content, not product photographs or firmware captures; no manufacturer image, logo or UI asset is bundled. Each mounted SVG uses unique gradient IDs so repeated previews remain independent. Appearance checked against Zoom's official product photos: [MS-70CDR](https://zoomcorp.com/en/jp/multi-effects/multistomp-pedals/ms-70cdr/), [MS-50G](https://zoomcorp.com/en/jp/multi-effects/multistomp-pedals/ms-50g/), [MS-60B](https://zoomcorp.com/en/jp/multi-effects/multistomp-pedals/ms-60b/) and [G1on](https://zoomcorp.com/en/us/multi-effects/guitar-effects/g1on/).

The press-kit images replaced the smaller elektronmods.com cut-outs (about 240×200 px; the Octatrack, Digitakt II, Digitone II and Model:Cycles ones were scaled-down copies of these press images) and four Commons photos on 21 October 2026. To add or refresh one: take the machine's top view without shadow, trim it to the machine's edges, scale it to fit 960×720 px and save it as WebP at quality 80 (each file is about 25–45 KB).

Files live in `public/machines/`. The app shows a credit line beside each machine's hero image and a full list wherever the machines are shown together: the overview (`#overview`, the landing page) and the forum's front page. Both use `MachinePhotoCredits` in `src/devices/DeviceImage.tsx`. Elektron product names identify the machines only; Modwerk is not affiliated with Elektron.
