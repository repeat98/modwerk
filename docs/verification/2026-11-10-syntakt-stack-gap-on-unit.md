# Syntakt 1.42 stack gap on a unit, 10 November 2026

The developer's Syntakt, results as reported after the run. The flashed file hashes to the stack-gap build recorded on 6 and 9 November. The unit booted, played with every sample slot already filled, survived three power cycles, and returned to stock 1.42 over USB. The samples were loaded before this flash. Loading or clearing them while this build was running was not done. The 84 bytes at `0x47fe0000` were not read. This run records that the lowered stack survived the session below. It does not prove those bytes remained.

- **Stock file:** `Syntakt_OS1.42.syx`, sha256 `ded3c59fdb909eb9e2c3307b30a486452c8fa6947b12c14e0ce35a187b7f37c1`. It stayed on this computer.
- **Build:** the copy at `Syntakt_OS_1.42/syntakt-1.42-stack-gap.syx` on this computer, sha256 `9152335cd89d976849aa50b9393ff3eb3a04549b01e9202add7f9de4ddfc51af`, the same hash as `/tmp/syntakt-stack-gap/syntakt-1.42-stack-gap.syx` and as the Node 24 rebuild in [the 9 November note](2026-11-09-syntakt-stack-gap-preflash.md). Flashed over USB with Elektron Transfer, in the normal upgrade, not from the startup menu. The copy stays out of git.
- **Boot:** the unit started and showed `MW01` at the bottom right of the screen, with `D` at the left. The startup animation ran. The screen then opened a new project whose pattern was empty. No exception screen, hang, or reboot was reported at boot.
- **Samples:** every slot was already filled with the Twinshot pack, loaded through Transfer before this flash. This build did not clear the slots. Slots were used on a couple of tracks. Scrolling through samples worked. Each slot was not played in turn.
- **Play:** about 20 minutes. Sequences on 7 tracks, different machines, machine settings, an LFO, and track mutes. No error on the screen, no hang, and no audio glitch were reported. The behaviour was reported as the same as stock.
- **Power cycles:** three, after the flash. Each startup matched the first, and each returned to the home screen.
- **Save:** the project was saved. After the power cycles the saved pattern was still there and played.
- **Back to stock:** stock 1.42 was sent back over USB. The unit boots on that OS and the saved pattern plays.
- **Not done:** playing an empty project before using the samples; clearing the slots and loading them again while this build was running; playing every sample on one track; playing 16 samples on 16 tracks; trig conditions; probabilities; the FX track sequencer; FILL; other patterns; song mode; the keyboard; retrig; parameter locks beyond velocity and length. The 84 bytes at `0x47fe0000` were not read. A DIN recovery of a unit that failed to boot was not needed: the normal screen was still available, and stock went back over USB. A full function pass was not done.
