# Analog Rytm MKII: the base's pad pressure — 22 November 2026

Pad Pressure Fix 0.2.0-experimental (Nathaniel Pavel, @npp1993; id `chromatic-aftertouch-fix`) moves into every variant of the Modwerk base, always on, with no setting: `base/pad-pressure.s` and four sites in `common` of `base.json`. The sites, their bytes and guards, and the code are the module's (its symbol renamed `mw_pad_pressure_out`, its constants `MW_PP_*`). New identities: send-bus **b485034b**, voices **1a8a1488**, plain **2977431b** (`sdk/machines/analog-rytm-mkii/base-binding.json`), each +44 B of `.run` (plain +3 B of `.bss`, alignment) and +4 sites, built by `npm run machine:base -- analog-rytm-mkii` with the reviewed toolchain, which reproduces the previous identities (46e2e4f6, e1f399a9, 5ca4480f) from the previous source.

- **Linker:** the module beside the new base is refused (`the mods do not combine`: the same sites), so no set can carry both. Each of the other 33 Rytm library modules links alone beside the new send-bus base.
- **Emulator** (rytm-emu `2ac0f1f`, headless `dtboot`, stock OS 1.74; each image cold-booted 10,000M with four NO presses; BD held 700M with raw pressure 20, 30, 28; `--count` on the aftertouch setter `0x4011f490`, the roll velocity writer `0x4011f262`, the performance amount `0x400b2c6a` and the four sites; `--midi-out`). The six cases in `sdk/machines/analog-rytm-mkii/tools/cases/pad-pressure-*.case` play BD through the link's `pad` (the panel's own pad and pressure messages) and read the sound's aftertouch word (0x800067de), the roll's velocity word (0x42ac6b4c), performance 1's amount (0x8000e57c) and USB MIDI out: plain pad (pressure 60, 30, 254: aftertouch `1e00`, `0f00`, `7f00`, poly pressure up to 127), RTRG held with VEL.CUR OFF (aftertouch and the roll's velocity both follow, poly pressure sent) and VEL.CUR 1 (aftertouch follows, the roll's velocity does not), ALWAYS ON with VEL.CUR OFF and 1, CHROMATIC (poly pressure on the played note 36, also after UP, none after the release; a roll's on its played note), PERF with RTRG held (performance 1 follows, back to 0).
