# Analog Rytm MKII: Improved AMP 0.1.0-experimental — 9 November 2026

Nathaniel Pavel's (@npp1993) Improved AMP is built as 0.1.0-experimental. On the AMP page HLD runs from no hold (0) to AUTO (127) and reads in steps at the current tempo and track speed; ATK, DEC and REL move in fine steps from 0.2 ms and read as times; OVR is finer at its low end and keeps its 0 to 127 number. ENV RESET gains AMP and AMP+FILT: a sequenced trig or retrig of such a sound is faded out over the 10 blocks (6.7 ms) before it, so the envelope restarts from silence without a click. It carries the envelope code first built as Improved Envelopes and the ENV RESET AMP of Iced-Cream's Env Reset Amp (MrScruff's rytm-mods 0015), which it replaces on a unit (`compatibility.conflicts`).

- **Emulator:** rytm-emu `2ac0f1f`. IA08, the earlier source's image: boot, the AMP page's DEC and HLD texts, ENV RESET's four values, the OVR reader's CV word at five knob values with every other register unchanged, and the pre-fade's mask before sequenced trigs, a pad roll's retrigs, swung trigs in the track's second event slot and the end of a retrig series. IA12 against IA08, run for run: the 112 frames of the AMP page and ENV RESET runs the same byte for byte, the fade mask, started bits and series words the same in all 19,400 readings ([TESTING.md](../../sdk/analog-rytm-mkii/modules/improved-amp/TESTING.md)). Host: 68 cases of the pre-fade's scan, and the old scan against the new on 20 million random states without a difference.
- **On a unit:** the author's automated USB test rig on their Analog Rytm MKII, as its reports have it. 11 November 2026, this source: 25 cases pass on IL04 (`662faad7…12b5`: the module alone on a development build of the base with a USB test link, loaded to RAM) against the references of 10 November; IA12, this package's own image, ran from RAM with MIDI notes and CCs only and read hold, decay, attack and OVR as IL04 did; a meter on the unit's timer put the module at about 1.8 points of the audio block before the change and 0.4 to 0.6 less after. 9 and 10 November, the earlier source on IL02 (`a569c9f5…284c`): the same 25 cases and the comparison with stock. Not run: a boot of the package's image from flash, a power cycle, pads, an external clock. The first note after a RAM load was near silent in some boots, also without the module; a 3 s wait avoided it.
