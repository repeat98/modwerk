# Analog Rytm MKII: Improved AMP 0.1.0-experimental — 9 November 2026

Nathaniel Pavel's (@npp1993) Improved AMP is built as 0.1.0-experimental. On the AMP page HLD runs from no hold (0) to AUTO (127) as a length in steps that follows the tempo and the track's speed, with FUNC + turn stopping on the step grid from 0.125 to 64 steps; ATK, DEC and REL move in fine steps from 0.2 ms and read as times; OVR is finer at its low end and keeps its 0 to 127 number. ENV RESET gains AMP and AMP+FILT: a sequenced trig or retrig of such a sound is faded out over the 10 blocks (6.7 ms) before it, so the envelope restarts from silence without a click. It carries the envelope code first built as Improved Envelopes and the ENV RESET AMP of Iced-Cream's Env Reset Amp (MrScruff's rytm-mods 0015), which it replaces on a unit (`compatibility.conflicts`).

- **On a unit:** the author's automated USB test rig on their Analog Rytm MKII, as its reports have it. 11 November 2026, this source: 25 cases pass on IL05 (`776adbab…1e82`: the module alone on a development build of the base with a USB test link, loaded to RAM), the references of the five cases rewritten for the hold in steps kept in that run after a look; one step held 125.0 ms at 120 BPM, 166.5 ms at 90 BPM and 83.0 ms with the track at 3/2x. Not run: an external MIDI clock (the unit did not take the clock it was sent), a tempo change during a hold, a boot of the package's image from flash, a power cycle, pads. The first note after a RAM load was near silent in some boots, also without the module; a 3 s wait avoided it.
