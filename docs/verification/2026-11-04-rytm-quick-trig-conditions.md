# Analog Rytm MKII: Quick Trig Conditions 0.2.0-experimental — 4 November 2026

Nathaniel Pavel's (@npp1993) Quick Fill Trig Condition, in `_submissions/` since #1149, enters the library as Quick Trig Conditions 0.2.0-experimental (id `quick-fill-trig-condition` unchanged). With a trig held in GRID RECORDING, FILL steps FILL, NOT FILL and no lock, as before; UP now steps a ratio condition's first number and DOWN its second, 1:2 to 8:8, in place of stock's trig + UP/DOWN, which opened the same RETRIG menu trig + RTRG opens. Its source-only OS 1.74 package is pinned in `sdk/imports/elekloader-quick-fill-trig-condition-2026-11-04.json` (commit `804e7374`).

The export reproduces with `package-elemod-recipe.mjs --check`, and `verify-elemod-release-exports.mjs` reproduces all 36 current exports from that build.
