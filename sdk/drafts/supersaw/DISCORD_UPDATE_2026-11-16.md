Quick SuperSAW-OT update: it now makes sound on my Octatrack MKII! 🎉

In private RAM trials on T1, I’ve tested tuning, detune, mix, oscillator count, stereo width and level. Sequencing also works in my tests, with short notes shaped by AMP HOLD/RELEASE. I tried P-locks and LFO modulation, including DETN/WIDE, with no audible clicks reported.

During the sequenced test, displayed total CPU readings were around 60%; the full session log peaked at 66.1%. These are whole-device readings, not an isolated SuperSAW CPU benchmark. The trial ended with a confirmed Undo and zero modules retained.

We also improved the test console after input and Undo issues. The latest cleanup succeeded, but the earlier intermittent refusals still need investigation.

Next: SuperSAW alongside sample tracks. This is still experimental: multi-track coexistence, Keep/reboot persistence and release qualification remain ahead. No installable release yet.

I’m a musician developing this with AI coding assistance. Thanks to the Modwerk community for making these experiments possible!
