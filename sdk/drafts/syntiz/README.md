# SYNTIZ (draft)

Status: **draft**. SYNTIZ is an Octatrack track machine with six models, whose sound goes through the track's
own filter, AMP envelope, LFOs and effects. All six have played on the author's MKII over USB, and a project
with SYNTIZ tracks came back whole after a power cycle (TESTING.md); no CPU reading on a unit yet.

The engine is original: no code, table or recording of any other product is used.

## The models

| Model | Sound | Its own controls |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SAW | a band-limited saw, an inner detuned pair and an outer pair at twice the detune | DET, UNI, WIDE |
| PULSE | a pulse with a width sweep, and a detuned pair | PW, PWM (depth), PWS (speed), DET, UNI |
| ENS | up to four notes of one shape (saw, square, narrow pulse), then a chorus | NOT2-4, SHAP, CHOR |
| TABLE | one of 32 single-cycle tables (512 points, 12 bits, read raw for their grain), a crossfade to the next, hard sync | TABL, MORF, MSWP, SYNC |
| MORPH | two of those tables mixed, the second up to two octaves above, each at its own resolution (12 bits to 1); changing a table crossfades | WAV1, MIX, WAV2, WID, TIME, BIT1, BIT2 |
| CHOIR | ENS with one of the tables for the shape: up to four notes, then the chorus | NOT2-4, TABL, CHOR |

Every model has DRIVE and RTRG (restart the phases at each trig), and every model but MORPH SUB (a square an
octave down) and SUB2 (a sine two octaves down). A sub below 25 Hz is silent, fading in up to 30 Hz.

The output stage is DRIVE's gain, a cubic soft clip with first-order antiderivative anti-aliasing (Parker,
Zavalishin and Le Bivic, DAFx 2016) after a small treble emphasis that gives back what the anti-aliasing
takes, and a DC blocker.

Panel layout (agreed with the author): choose SYNTIZ in the machine chooser, then a double tap of the track key
opens the model list, as Analog BD does for 808/909. PTCH stays on SRC A with FLEX's scale (about
+/-1 octave, five steps a semitone), so chromatic keys, scales, LFOs and locks work as on FLEX; OCT on
SRC SETUP picks the register (the author's choice, 27 October 2026).

| | SRC A | B | C | D | E | F | SETUP A | B | C | D | E | F |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SAW | PTCH | DET | UNI | WIDE | SUB | SUB2 | FINE | OCT | DRIV | RTRG | | |
| PULSE | PTCH | PW | PWM | PWS | SUB | SUB2 | FINE | OCT | DRIV | RTRG | DET | UNI |
| ENS | PTCH | NOT2 | NOT3 | NOT4 | SHAP | CHOR | FINE | OCT | DRIV | RTRG | SUB | SUB2 |
| TABLE | PTCH | TABL | MORF | MSWP | SYNC | SUB | FINE | OCT | DRIV | RTRG | SUB2 | |
| MORPH | PTCH | WAV1 | MIX | WAV2 | WID | TIME | FINE | OCT | DRIV | RTRG | BIT1 | BIT2 |
| CHOIR | PTCH | NOT2 | NOT3 | NOT4 | TABL | CHOR | FINE | OCT | DRIV | RTRG | SUB | SUB2 |

FINE, OCT, DRIV and RTRG sit on the same SETUP encoders in every model. PTCH neutral at OCT 0 plays C3.

Chromatic keys, MIDI notes and recorded notes cover two octaves, as on FLEX: a recorded note is a one-byte
PTCH lock on FLEX's scale. OCT places those two octaves anywhere from three octaves down to three up.
A wider keyboard that records would need another PTCH unit, which would put the Scale Quantizer and LFO
depths on PTCH out of step with FLEX: the author chose to keep FLEX's (28 October 2026).

TABLE's 32 tables are the author's own set, computed by `tools/make_tables.py` from formulas in four families of eight (geometric, additive, digital, resonant); none is taken or
fitted from any instrument. TABL, WAV1 and WAV2 show the table's name.

## Files

- `engine.h`, `engine.c`: the oscillators, integer only, no 64-bit products (the ColdFire multiplies 32 x 32 -> 32).
- `tables.h` (generated by `tools/make_tables.py`): the 32 tables TABLE, MORPH and CHOIR read.
- `test_engine.c`: host checks (`check`) and the listening set (`render <dir>`).
- `machine_map.h`, `machine_map.c`, `test_machine_map.c`: the twelve knob bytes per model, and the played pitch
  from the PTCH word on stock's curve, FINE and OCT.
- `bench_cf.c`, `tools/count_cf.sh`: instruction counts on a ColdFire under qemu.

See [TESTING.md](TESTING.md) for what has been measured.
