## Budget defect and correction

`3871d90e345db70977f247185fdc121085e4026215e8c089840e3ba5e2a9d9a5`
failed the fixed procedure's T1 price check: quiet 29.1%, triggered 53.0%,

passed; its 162-second trial logged no new crash, stall, freeze or skipped track.
T5 and multiple FRACT owners were refused, as its intentional policy requires.
The generic procedure assumes T1/T5 and eight instances; those cases do not
establish a defect in this one-owner T1–T4 module, nor a procedure pass.

## Probe limits and further checks

The standard `guard` read resets the base frame max/count/busy counters. The
first collection spanning that reset correctly failed its coherence flags and
was retained. Subsequent captures wait three seconds after each reset. Their
base frame maxima are **since the named guard reset**, despite the decoder's
historical `baseFramePeakSinceBootUs` name. Own callback peaks remain lifetime
since probe installation and include native FLEX plus timer/call overhead.
Timer counts are not ColdFire CPU cycles; the shared guard also pays base work.

A separate 120.134-second 16-bit stereo trial with sixteen trigs and moving MAIN,
PAN and SRNG controls observed no skips, crash, stall or freeze. Its inclusive
own-block peak was 180.864 microseconds. This did not include programmed locks
or LFO modulation, so the full requested stress case remains open.

## Corrected build and stress failures

Development normal package 54d4 passed self-test 1/1, all 12 Part switches and
standard Undo with exact inventory/Part restoration. Its price test measured
29.4% quiet and 53.0% triggered, a 23.6-point delta within declared 40. The generic
procedure still failed at its T5 case: this module intentionally supports one
owner on T1–T4. Additional owners were correctly refused. This is not a full
procedure pass.

The separate corrected probe
`18d3f996f03b770d3be73c6986878e6e988ad4d89b22d07f21c23b793817362f`
completed all sixteen matched four-format arms. Extreme-minus-quiet means were
36.1/37.5/36.9/38.1 points for 16m/16s/24m/24s. No new skips were observed in those
arms; maximum whole-interrupt frame peak was 98.4%. This repeats the budget
measurement, without proving modulation/streaming headroom.

The subsequent normal 54d4 stress used an owned disposable project, 64 T1 trigs,
64 verified alternating native parameter-lock rows, three verified native LFOs
and moving MAIN/PAN/SRNG controls. The sounding source was 16-bit mono, 88,200
frames at 44.1 kHz. Published FM Synth was installed but unused; this does not
qualify neighbouring-machine playback or Part reload.

| USB audio layout | Observed skipped T1 renders | Failure capture frame peak | Result |

| 20 channels | 734 over the trial; 256 at first detector | 106.3% | Failed |

The four-channel failure was detected after about 58 seconds. No new crash or
DSP stall was observed. CPU allowance 45, booked 40 and guard share 80% were not
changed. Price metadata alone therefore does not fix the performance defect.
The cause is not isolated; it cannot be attributed solely to 20-channel audio.
Both private recordings lasted 15.0117 seconds with nonzero MAIN. CUE was not
routed and stayed silent, so neither recording proves CUE audibility or a clean
audio-path pass. The full 120-second modulated stress did not pass.

The first USB-audio removal disconnected the host link and left recovery open.
After reopening only this session’s link and selecting stock FLEX in all four
Parts of the owned scratch bank, standard recovery succeeded. The final retry’s
Undo then completed normally, generation 10, inventory empty.
