## First attempt and correction

The initial helper confirmed all 19 inventory rows, then requested the cached
whole-set digest. The base deliberately withholds that request while a complete
trial retains its whole-set rows. The helper treated the expected busy result
as a failure, then sent only one rollback request. The loader restores one row
per acknowledged request and reports needs-recovery between rows; 18 remained
after that first request. This was a host orchestration error.

No further installation was attempted on that state. The owner power-cycled,
and the original base, exact active-set identity and all 19 module IDs/layouts/
names were verified before retrying. The corrected helper follows the normal
client's bounded retry of needs-recovery and unsafe responses. During the trial
it verifies the acknowledged upload digest/phase/length and complete inventory,
without claiming that the unavailable canonical digest was read. The same
reviewed base and packages were then loaded successfully.
