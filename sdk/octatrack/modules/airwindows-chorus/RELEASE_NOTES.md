# 0.1.0-experimental — 8 October 2026

- First release of Chris Johnson’s MIT-licensed Airwindows Chorus as an Octatrack FX2 module; original source and full attribution retained.
- Smooth SPD, RNG and MIX per sample, preserve the original air compensation and three-point delay read, and initialize dirty rings without a long synchronous clear.
- Owner reports 50 minutes of successful MKII use with several instances and full knob sweeps on the first image. Fresh hardware testing of the bounded-init image is waived; eight-track timing, locks/scenes/LFOs and physical persistence remain unverified.
- Delay precision is approximately 16 bits; extreme modulation retains pitch and aliasing artifacts.
