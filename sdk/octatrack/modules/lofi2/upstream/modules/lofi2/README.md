# `lofi2` -- LOFI2

One modulator, ring mod or a stereo frequency shifter, followed by sample-rate and bit reduction on stock LO-FI's curves and a high-shelf cut.

An insert on a free id (`0x1f`; `0x1e` until the October port), so it displaces no stock effect: stock LO-FI stays in the chooser beside it. Signal chain: `dry -> modulator -> MIX -> SRR -> BRR -> LPF -> out`. MIX is the modulator's depth, not a global wet/dry: SRR and BRR run after it and stay live at MIX 0, as they do in stock LO-FI. The defaults are a passthrough (MIX, FREQ, FDBK, SRR, BRR at 0, AMPH at its centre, LPF at 127).

How it differs from CHARACTER, which replaces stock LO-FI and also crushes and ring-modulates: LOFI2 is built around the modulator. It adds a frequency shifter, gives the ring mod an MF-102-style exponential FREQ with a LOW/HIGH range and LO-FI's L/R phase offset, and matches stock LO-FI's SRR rates and BRR curve (our own code, measured against stock) so measurements and note tables made against stock LO-FI still apply. CHARACTER is a channel-strip chain (crush, fold, saturation, compression, width).

## Knobs

| page | slot | name | range | what it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 | FREQ | 0-127 | modulator frequency, exponential over six octaves from RANGE's base; read by whichever MODE is selected; keeps an LFO's fraction between steps and glides, so sweeps are smooth |
| 1 | 1 | MIX | 0-127 | modulator depth: crossfades the ring or shift output against the dry |
| 1 | 2 | FDBK / GAIN | 0-127 | one knob, read per MODE, and the panel renames it. SHIFT: FDBK, the feedback ladder (f, f+d, f+2d), capped at 0.40 (0.45 until #825, where the top of FDBK self-oscillated). RING: GAIN on the ring product, 2^(k/64): 0 dB at 0 (today's level), +6 dB at 64, +11.9 dB at 127 |
| 1 | 3 | SRR | 0-127 | sample-rate reduction, a fractional hold at stock LO-FI's measured rates: no reduction through 7, 8,018 Hz at 64, 3,564 Hz at 127; the rate glides, so turns and LFOs sweep smoothly |
| 1 | 4 | BRR | 0-127 | bit-depth reduction, a continuous round-to-nearest quantiser on stock LO-FI's curve: the step grows from 1 LSB through 3 to 2^13.5 at 64 and 2^20.8 at 127, exactly stock's; it glides, so turns and LFOs sweep smoothly |
| 1 | 5 | LPF | 0-127 | high-shelf cut, -12 dB above a corner swept exponentially from 100 Hz at 0 to 16 kHz at 126 (about 17 steps an octave); 127 takes it out of circuit, bit for bit; it glides, so turns and LFOs sweep smoothly |
| 2 | 6 | RANGE | LOW / HIGH | FREQ's base: RING 2 Hz / 60 Hz (MF-102), SHIFT 2 Hz / 31.25 Hz (Bode 1630, six octaves to 2 kHz) |
| 2 | 8 | MODE | RING / DOWN / UP | ring mod, or the shifter moving every partial down or up; mutually exclusive |
| 2 | 10 | AMPH | -64..+63 | ring carrier's L/R phase offset, -90 to +88.6 degrees (1.4 a step), 0 at the centre; + puts R ahead, - puts L ahead; RING only |

## Design

- **Ring mod** is not stock's: WarpFold's parabolic carrier `4p(1-|p|)` under an exponential six-octave taper (the MF-102's ranges, 2-130 Hz and 60-4,000 Hz, are each exactly six octaves). An exponential taper has no zero: FREQ 0 is the base frequency, not silence. One knob step is 6/128 octave (56 cents), so the knob word's LFO fraction (bits 15-8) is kept, and the carrier step glides to the block's value (about 1.5 ms, see the glides below), for the ring mod and the shifter alike.
- **The shifter is stereo**: two Hilbert allpass pairs (Olli Niemitalo's 90-degree pair: eight sections `y[n] = a^2 (x[n] + y[n-2]) - x[n-2]`, his coefficients squared) sharing one oscillator, rather than BodeShift's analytic pair on the mono sum, which would collapse the image at MIX 127. Direction is the carrier's: DOWN runs the oscillator backwards (sin is odd, cos even, so I·cos + Q·sin(−t) is the lower sideband), so UP/DOWN costs nothing in the loop; the step in use takes the same sign at a MODE change, made only once the wet is fully ducked, so the carrier never glides through 0 Hz. The chains are pointer-walked, which lets each section carry parallel moves, and their state is split by sample parity: each section is an allpass in z^-2, so even and odd samples are independent recursions, and each parity keeps only its own [y[n-2], x[n-2]], overwritten in place with [y[n], x[n]]. That halves the state traffic of one shifting four-word set with the same arithmetic in the same order (bit-identical to it): 6 words a section, against 9 with the shifting set and 21 with displaced addressing.
- **SRR** is our own fractional sample-and-hold: a phase advances by `inc` a sample and the held pair is re-latched on each wrap. `inc` comes from the module's own table (`SRR_INC`, 129 words, carried by `DspSection.ptable`), indexed by the knob and interpolated by the fraction an LFO leaves in the knob word, and it glides to its target per sample (about 1.5 ms), so a turn does not step at the block rate. The rates are stock LO-FI's as measured on the unit (10 Oct 2026: a 1 kHz tone through each over USB, the rate read from the images): 8,018 Hz at 64 and 3,564 Hz at 127, a hold of L(k) = (k+128)^2/4096 - 3.5 samples, floored at 1. The September port of stock's code held twice as long at every setting (4,009 Hz at 64, 1,782 Hz at 127).
- **BRR** is our own quantiser: `y = round(x * 2^-E) * 2^E`, E the step above the LSB in bits. E = 23(1 - (1 - 0.78125 k/128)^2) - 1, floored at 0, stock LO-FI's curve (measured against stock from DC plateaus, 10 Oct 2026: within 0.01 bit at every setting from 16 to 127; the -1 is the bit stock's own arithmetic carries), from the module's own table (`BRR_E`, 129 words after `SRR_INC` in the same `ptable`), interpolated by the knob word's LFO fraction and gliding per sample (1/64 a sample, about 1.5 ms). E's whole bits are a variable shift; its fraction (to 1/128 bit) indexes one 129-word table, `m = 2^-(j/128)`, read from both ends: `1/(2m) = 2^((j-128)/128)` is the entry at 128 - j, so the step's mantissa and its reciprocal are a pair to 1.4e-7 and the gain below the clip is unity at every setting. At BRR 0 the held sample passes untouched. A rounded sample a step above full scale is caught by the limited move into the shelf.
- **LPF** is our own first-order high shelf: `y = x - kd (x - A x)/2`, A a first-order allpass with coefficient c. With kd = 3/4 that is unity at DC and 1/4 (-12.04 dB) at Nyquist, the depth the ported stock shelf had at every setting (measured 10 Oct 2026). A first-order shelf has no resonance, so its gain is at most 1 at every frequency: safe inside a sound-on-sound feedback loop, which is where it is used. c comes from the module's own table (`LPF_C`, 129 words at +387 in the `ptable`): the allpass break frequency (the shelf's -2.7 dB point; -6 dB is about twice it) runs exponentially from 100 Hz at 0 to 16 kHz at 126, interpolated by the knob word's LFO fraction. The ported shelf used stock EQUALIZER's frequency mapping, which left the corner under 100 Hz for the whole bottom half of the knob (a flat 12 dB cut there) and packed the sweep into the top 40 values. At 127, kd goes to 0 and the output is the input bit for bit; the ported shelf was still -1.8 dB at 15 kHz there, which compounds on every pass of a feedback loop. c and kd glide per sample (1/64 a sample, snapping onto the target). The allpass feeds the bits its store drops back into the next sample (first-order error feedback), so rounding cannot build a dead band when c is near 1: the ported shelf's impulse response summed to 0.32 at LPF 0 where it should be 1. No stock table is read, so payload A and B run the same numbers.
- **Every continuous knob glides.** FREQ, MIX, GAIN/FDBK, SRR, BRR, LPF and AMPH all keep the knob word's LFO fraction (bits 15-8) and glide to the block's values. All but BRR go through `lf_gset`, which works out each one's step once per block, a one-pole at the block rate ((63/64)^16 kept per block, the old per-sample glide's own rate, about 1.5 ms) spread over the block as a linear ramp, and the loop only adds it (4 words a glide, no call). When the step rounds to nothing the value snaps onto the target, so 0, unity and the LPF bypass are exact. At block ends the values track the old per-sample glide to within about 90 LSB. BRR's E keeps the per-sample one-pole (1/64 a sample, inline, about 12 cycles): its step is mostly noise, and its turn gate reads a statistic of that noise, so its trajectory is kept exactly. That gate is close to its own noise floor: the 654-cycle source also fails it with a 97.1 Hz tone (-52.9 dBFS), the excess landing at no fixed block position. verify_lofi2 turns each one 20 -> 110 under -70 dBFS.
- **Cost, and how many fit.** `cycle_count.py` counts 416 cycles/sample at the dearest path (SHIFT; 654 before the October rework below); RING is about 240. That count is a floor: on the chip a register or `(rn)` move costs 2, a one-word `r7`-displaced move 4 and a two-word one 6 (CHIP.md section 2, probe 57), so the word count understates displaced-heavy code. Weighting the SHIFT loop by those costs (two-word immediates and calls guessed at 4 and 8) gives about 1,530 for the September source, 1,265 at 506 counted cycles, and about 995 now: about 35% below September. On the unit (11 Oct 2026, image 99, the September source) three SHIFT UP instances on core 0 (T5-T7) played and a fourth (T8) cut the audio out with bursts of digital noise, a core out of cycles; T1-T7 in RING also played. Four in SHIFT on core 1 (T1-T4) was not run then (Bryan T, 12 Oct: the earlier note that it was is withdrawn). At 506 counted cycles (12 Oct, `_bt_oct` build 10) four in SHIFT cut out on either core, which the weighting predicts: 4 x 1,265 is above 3 x 1,530, the most core 0 has been seen to carry. 4 x 990 is about 12% below it, and at 414 counted cycles (12 Oct, `_bt_oct` build 11; 416 since, the parity toggle's EOR moved to the register form stock uses, #704 review) all eight tracks ran SHIFT at once, four on each core, switching between UP and DOWN while playing, cleanly. **SHIFT: up to four per core, all eight tracks.** At 416 (`_bt_oct` build 12, 12 Oct, the review's EOR form): eight in UP, eight in DOWN and eight in RING, each played cleanly. What FX1 held on those tracks was not recorded; an FX1 effect on the same track takes from the same core.
- **Image rejection.** The unwanted sideband sits about 45-55 dB under the wanted one across the band (analytic, ideal mixer: -49 dB at 100 Hz, -44 at 300 Hz, -55 at 1 kHz, -49 at 3 kHz, -51 at 5 kHz, -50 at 10 kHz, -46 at 15 kHz); verify_lofi2 measures it under `dsp_host` at 1, 3, 5, 10 and 15 kHz. Until 13 Oct 2026 the sections were loaded with Niemitalo's a's unsquared, a transcription error inherited from the September port and found by a model of the structure (13 Oct 2026): the I/Q difference was off 90 degrees and the image came up to -19 to -22 dB at 3-5 kHz and 15 kHz, -30 at 1 and 10 kHz. The old gate tested only 1 and 10 kHz against -25 dB, which that set passed. SHIFT renders before and after differ; RING, SRR, BRR and LPF do not.
- `init` zeroes the whole instance block (`$00..$83`), then starts the wet fully ducked, so a fresh instance fades in.
- **GAIN** makes up the ring path's level. The ring product sits 2.73 dB under the dry (the parabolic carrier's RMS is sqrt(8/15)), and a sub-audio tremolo reads lower still (its envelope averages 2/3). GAIN is 2^(k/64), computed per block as `2^n * 2^f` with a quadratic for `2^f` (exact at 0 and 1, within 0.023 dB between), stored as GAIN/4 and applied with `asl #2`, so GAIN 0 is bit-exact unity. Above unity a hot input clips at the store's limiter.
- **AMPH** is bipolar: the knob word less `$400000` runs from -1/4 cycle (knob 0, -90 degrees) to 63/256 cycle (knob 127, +88.6 degrees) in phase units. Every distinct stereo motion of a ring mod lives inside +-90 degrees: offsets of theta and 180-theta give the same envelope with lead and lag swapped, and the sign says which channel leads.
- **The MODE duck.** A MODE change swaps one modulator for an unrelated one, or flips Q's sign, which is a step in the output. The wet is ducked to dry over 5 ms, the running mode switches only once it is fully out (at the next block boundary), and the wet returns over 5 ms. The flags read the running mode, never the knob, and MIX runs at m*(1-e), exactly m when nothing is ducking.

## Status

- Flashed and heard on Bryan T's MKII in September 2026, built on the tree of 12 Sep 2026.
- Ported to this tree in October 2026. Three changes the port required:
  - the shifter's chain pointer was `lua (r7+$40)`, which assembles to `r7-$40` (`lua` takes a displacement of -64..63; docs/contributing/TRAPS.md). All four Hilbert chains had been keeping their state in the 64 words below the instance's own block. This tree's build refuses it (the disassemble-what-you-assemble round-trip); the pointer is now `r7 + n1`. It moved where the state lives, not the arithmetic: under `rig_render` (T1 and T5, every MODE), the September source and this one render bit-identically, because nothing in that layout uses those 64 words (the top of the slot below, `$c0..$ff`). On the unit it was a latent corruption of whatever does.
  - every page-1 knob read is masked (an LFO-modulated knob word carries bits 8-15; docs/contributing/TRAPS.md): `and #>$7f0000` for the selects, and, since the October rewrites, `and #>$7fff00` for the continuous knobs, which keep those bits as the LFO's fraction.
  - `init` zeroes the block (verify_dirty_state), where it had been a bare `rts`.
- Rendered against the September source in this tree (the same image built from each, `rig_render`, T1 on payload B and T5 on payload A; passthrough, RING with and without AMPH, SRR/BRR/LPF, SHIFT UP, SHIFT DOWN with FDBK): bit-identical in every case, and T1 bit-identical to T5.
- The port ran on Bryan T's MKII as image 96 (remix `_bt_oct`, his own: LOFI2 with RECORDER LOOP FIX, USB AUDIO IN ABCD and OUT MAIN CUE), 10 Oct 2026: LPF right on track 1 (payload B), SHIFT on two tracks at once, MODE through RING / DOWN / UP without a fault.
- AMPH bipolar, GAIN and the MODE duck ran on the unit as image 97 (October 2026).
- Our own SRR, BRR and LPF ran on the unit as image 98 (10 Oct 2026), on track 1 (payload B) and track 5 (payload A): "sounding and working great", and the MODE change no longer clicks.
- The FREQ, MIX, GAIN/FDBK and AMPH glides ran on the unit as image 99 (10 Oct 2026), with five LOFI2 instances running at once (so at least three on one core): no issues. Image 99, 11 Oct 2026: seven in RING (T1-T7) and seven in SHIFT UP (T1-T7) played cleanly; eight in SHIFT UP overran core 0 (see Design, "Cost, and how many fit").

## Open

- Stock LO-FI follows its hold with a fixed linear-phase FIR near Nyquist (seen in its step response: symmetric pre-ringing, 7-10 samples of latency); LOFI2 does not, and at stock's rates it is not what makes stock's SRR sound smoother.
- Under `dsp_host`, stock LO-FI clips its input at +-0.5 FS at every BRR setting, 0 included (a +-0.95 FS ramp, 10 Oct 2026). Not checked on the unit, where the input may arrive scaled; LOFI2 does not copy it, and its BRR gate stays inside +-0.45 FS.
- On page 2, MODE names itself (RING / DOWN / UP in the name field) and the turn-flash prints the same word beneath it; RANGE and AMPH show their values only while turned.
- Tuning by ear: whether six exponential octaves suit the knob, and the SHIFT range pair.
- SHIFT's cost: four on one core did not fit at 654 or at 506 counted cycles. The October rework, 654 -> 416 counted (about 1,530 -> 995 weighted): per-block glide steps for all but BRR, walked by a pointer with the stores in parallel slots; `mpy y1,x1` in the Hilbert sections; the parity-split Hilbert state; I·cos formed between the chains instead of parked; the shelf state and every other per-sample slot below $40; the direction moved into the carrier; the sine inlined; values kept in registers where they were stored and reloaded. Every step but the per-block glides and the direction is bit-identical to the one before it. On the unit at 414 (the EOR's form aside, the same loop): eight SHIFT instances, four per core (build 11, 12 Oct 2026). Untried: the Hilbert coefficients from Y memory in a parallel slot (about 2 words a section, but LOFI2 owns no Y today), and a quadrature oscillator in place of the two sines (not bit-identical).
- From the same model (13 Oct 2026, not measured here): the parabolic sine's carrier-harmonic spurs sit near -63 dBc (a 1024-entry interpolated table would reach about -104 dBc, at a cost in cycles and table space; LOFI2 owns no Y memory), and the Hilbert sections' truncating stores leave a limit cycle near -100 dBFS after the input stops (rounding toward zero removes it, at a few instructions a section).
- On the unit: the image at -20 dB was part of how SHIFT sounded on images 96-99 and builds 11-12. Whether the cleaner shift is preferred is an ear decision.
