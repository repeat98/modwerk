USB audio and MIDI contributions (USB Audio 0.2)

Source: sambanks/octabam at 7b2984c859732ae6c797ae49c7d61d250b1b6519.
The applicable full MIT copyright and licence notices for Octabam and
octemu are retained in sdk/octabam/licenses/ and the third-party notice bundle.

Mark Roberts (@markandrus / octemu): original USB audio and MIDI
implementations, descriptors, audio producer, packet builder and rate control.

Bryan Tysinger (@bryantysinger): MAIN/CUE channel layouts, stream latency
and alignment work including CUE alignment with MASTER TRACK, and upstream
hardware measurements of the shared USB audio stack.

Sam Banks (@sambanks): Octabam port and output layouts, USB MIDI receive
FIFO and clock timestamp fixes, shared audio/MIDI interrupt integration,
bus reset and session-end handling.

allmyfriendsaresynths (@clickysteve): fixed-rate UAC2 SET_CUR handshake
for Outbox 8 compatibility, post-fader track layout following LEVEL,
mute, solo and the crossfader, and upstream hardware measurements.

Modwerk: local setup, routing plan and browser integration.
