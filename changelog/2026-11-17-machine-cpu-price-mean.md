SDK: a machine's CPU price is now what one playing track adds to the frame interrupt, not its longest single render. Measured on an Octatrack MKII, FM Synth adds 4.6 points a track while its longest render is 26.6 % of a frame; priced by the peak, the fourth FM Synth track was refused although eight played fine. The frame guard still covers the peaks. A ported machine (FM Synth, POLY8, VECTOR, Analog BD) can now carry a price: `"cpuPrice"` in its `machine-ports.json` entry. No module's package changes with this; each machine gets its price in its own release.
