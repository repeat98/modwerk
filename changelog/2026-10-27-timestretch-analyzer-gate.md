Octatrack base: tracks with TSTR set to OFF no longer run the stock time-stretch analysis. Stock runs it for every playing Flex or Static track on every audio frame, even when the track never stretches, at about 1 to 1.4% of the CPU per track. Tracks with time-stretch on, BEAT included, and recorder loops work exactly as before. Thanks to Bryan T for measuring it and Zac Kyoti for mapping it.
