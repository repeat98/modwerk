Octatrack base 0.8.2: recording tracks cost less CPU. While a track records, the Octatrack updates its recorder's control record about one and a half times every audio frame, with six writes into a slow part of its memory, and two of those writes almost always store the value already there. The base now leaves those two out when nothing changes, so the recorder's memory ends exactly as stock leaves it. On Bryan T's MKII the same two skips took the routine from 48.0 to 36.4 µs a frame with seven tracks recording; this base has not been measured on a unit yet. Idea and measurements: Bryan T.
