Diagnostics: when a USB sync stops because the Octatrack did not confirm a transfer, a shared report now also carries six of the Octatrack's own USB counts over that sync: transfers received and refused, USB resets, and data stages started, completed and abandoned. The error type alone could not tell whether the Octatrack refused each transfer at once, a transfer stopped part way, or the connection was reset, and that is what decides the fix for syncs that fail every time on a few units. The counts are read only while a sync runs, and the privacy page names the addition.
