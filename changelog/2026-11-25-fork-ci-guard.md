Forks: syncing a fork's `main` no longer runs the Worker deploy or the site publish there. Both workflows ran on every push to `main` in any repository, failed in a fork for want of the secrets, and mailed the fork's owner a failure for something they never started (seen by a contributor on 23 November). The first job of `worker.yml` and `pages.yml` now runs only in `repeat98/modwerk`, and the jobs after it are skipped with it.
