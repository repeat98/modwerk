Base (Octatrack): a machine's DSP voice now books each effect slot at what the bank's Parts use there, as effects do, instead of the dearest stock effect in every slot. Core 0 keeps up to 3,120 cycles for voices instead of 384, so a voice such as BlackPyramids' can fit beside a light bank. A bank or project load that needs more room drops voices from that core's last track down, and those tracks render silence with a DSP FULL box until there is room. `build_core.py --measure-voices` (development twins only) admits voices without a cycle charge so their hardware price can be measured (#1542). Takes effect with the next base.
