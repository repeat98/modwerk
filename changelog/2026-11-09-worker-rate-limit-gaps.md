Accounts: password-reset emails have their own daily budget, so a flood of sign-ups can no longer stop recovery mail, and sign-ups are capped per hour across the site. Ten wrong passwords now lock out only the address they came from, not the account everywhere.
Community: download and usage counts are limited per connection as well as per visitor, so public download numbers cannot be inflated. Fresh module statistics are limited per connection, and the shared answer is served past that. Issue reports check your own limit before the site-wide one, so one member's refused reports no longer block everyone else's.
