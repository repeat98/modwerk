Developer docs: the Analog Rytm guide adds what Control In Actions found in the CONTROL INPUT menus on OS 1.74. It covers the hidden footswitch mode and its rows, the menu's own copy of the settings and when stock saves it, and that a learn's NO leaves the new levels to be saved on leaving. It also covers how the screen redraws and draws its bars, how the FOOTSW DEST names are looked up, and how FILL latches from FUNC plus a double press. It notes that STOP ends live recording.
