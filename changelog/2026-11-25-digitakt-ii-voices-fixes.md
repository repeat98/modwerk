Digitakt II base: linked voices fixes. The SETTINGS > VOICES row now names the track you are on every time SETTINGS opens (it kept the track that was on when the menu was first built, so it could read T9 on any track), and it has an icon like the other SETTINGS rows, with the count beside it. A chord note from the keys or MIDI on a voice track now always plays the lead's sound: after the unit had forgotten which sound a track last loaded (a kit change, a sound lock), it could load the voice's own unedited sound with the lead's machine. When VOICES is lowered while notes ring or fade, a track that leaves the group keeps the lead's machine and ends its note there until it plays a note of its own, which brings back its own sound: before, its note's release could cut the lead's next note short, and its own machine could take over the fading note and play the lead's sample (a kick heard on the first hit). With every track at 1 nothing changes.
