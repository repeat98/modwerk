Digitakt II base: linked voices fixes. The SETTINGS > VOICES row now names the track you are on every time SETTINGS opens (it kept the track that was on when the menu was first built, so it could read T9 on any track), and it has an icon like the other SETTINGS rows, with the count beside it. When VOICES is lowered while notes ring, a former voice track's ringing note now ends where it plays: before, its release went to the lead, so it could cut the lead's next note short and play it without its own envelope, while the former voice's note rang on. A track that leaves a group also plays its own sound again from its next note, not the lead's settings it had been given. With every track at 1 nothing changes.
