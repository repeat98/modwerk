Octatrack base (beta): with MIDI clock arriving on DIN and over USB at once, the Octatrack follows DIN alone. Both were counted before, so the tempo read about double and was saved into the project. While DIN clock runs, USB clock, START, STOP, CONTINUE and song position are ignored. USB takes over about a sixth of a second after DIN's last clock. Checked in code tests only, not yet on a unit.
