Development: `npm run check` now knows who holds which battery RAM on the Octatrack. `sdk/machines/octatrack/battery-ram.json` lists the ranges held by the base, Play Modes and the Micro Timing draft. The check refuses two ranges that overlap, and any address that the base, a module or a draft names in stock's free range outside a range its owner holds. Before, two users of the same battery RAM went unnoticed: the new module store first took the Micro Timing draft's table.
