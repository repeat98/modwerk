Octatrack (USB, beta): a module machine such as Analog BD now plays right after a power cycle. Its sound engine loads while the sequencer runs when it fits in free DSP memory, instead of waiting for you to stop, and it loads several times faster. MODULES LOADED now appears once those engines are running, so pressing PLAY on it sounds every track. If your effects fill the DSP so that one of them has to move first, the engine still waits until the Octatrack has been stopped for two seconds. A halt of the DSPs that can happen in the first minute after a cold boot (DSP STOPPED, then no module engine loads until the next power cycle) is older than this change and not fixed by it.
