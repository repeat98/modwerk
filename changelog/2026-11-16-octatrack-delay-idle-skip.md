Octatrack base 0.7.6: the ColdFire no longer reads, filters, mixes and writes back the delay memory of tracks whose delay has nothing to play. Stock did all of that for all eight tracks every frame, whatever the track's FX2: 45 µs of every 363. A track with no DELAY, or with a DELAY that has not been sent to since the unit started, now gets only its dry level, and the sound is bit-identical. On an MKII with DELAY on every track at its defaults the CPU meter dropped from 41 % to 34 %.
