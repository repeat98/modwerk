Octatrack base 0.7.6: the ColdFire no longer reads, filters, mixes and writes back the delay memory of tracks whose delay has nothing to play. Stock did all of that for all eight tracks every frame, whatever the track's FX2: 45 µs of every 363. A track with no DELAY, or whose DELAY has faded out with its send at zero, now gets only its dry level. What this leaves out is below -120 dBFS: stock's delay never fades to exact zero, so it keeps a trace far below hearing. On an MKII with DELAY on every track at its defaults the CPU meter dropped from 41 % to 34 %.
