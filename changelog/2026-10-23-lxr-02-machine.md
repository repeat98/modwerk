Machines: the Erica Synths LXR-02 has a machine page. Erica Synths published its firmware and bootloader source under GPL-3.0 on 23 October 2026, so it gets its own family (`erica`) and road to mods: Firmware source builds, Marked build boots, Modwerk base with hooks and First mods, with nothing to reverse-engineer. It is in research (the first step is started), credited to Erica's two repositories, grouped as Erica Synths in the machine menu and shown with Erica Synths' top-view product image, credited (it carries no licence; see docs/MACHINE_IMAGES.md). It stays locked for modules until a base and an `lxr` module contract exist; the plan, the licence and trademark terms a build must meet, and the owner's open decisions are in [docs/LXR_02.md](docs/LXR_02.md). The independence notices now name Erica Synths too. Checked with contract, registry and overview tests; the page was not looked at in a browser, and the source was not built here.
