Octatrack base (beta): two opt-in machine flags. SETUP_ALL lets SRC SETUP's encoder E edit a module machine's fifth setup control; the base kept that encoder read-only for every module machine, a rule taken from POLY8's hidden slot, so Analog BD's HIGH never moved over USB. LIST makes a machine without a pool open its track's double-tap window on the machine list, as PICKUP's does, instead of an empty sample list (and with no flicker). POLY8, VECTOR, FM Synth and Analog BD 0.1.5 are unchanged; Analog BD 0.1.6 sets both. Checked in the host test, the emulator and on a MKII (flashed, with 14 modules).
