Octatrack base (development twins only): an FX effect's DSP code and its ColdFire half can exchange blocks every audio frame, for effects whose delay lines are longer than the DSP's memory, like most of the Quadraverb's programs. A module asks for it in its `ot_dsp_effects` record (halfwords a frame each way, and two callbacks); the base gives each slot that runs it an in-block and an out-block on the DSP, fills and takes them every frame with a fixed delay of three frames, and names them in the slot's record only once the first blocks have arrived. It is built only into development twins (`stream_probe.py --stream-channel`), for all eight tracks: tracks 5-8 at the times the probes already took, tracks 1-4 right after stock's own transfers to their DSP core. It has not run on a unit yet.
