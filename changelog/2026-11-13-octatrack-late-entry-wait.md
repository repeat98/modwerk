Octatrack base (beta): a frame interrupt that starts 110 to 190 µs late now waits until it is 190 µs late, at most 80 µs. Upstream octabam (Sam Banks) measured that a late start inside that window, right after the one long frame at START or a program change, could stop the audio until a power cycle. The wait is not yet measured on a Modwerk base: it takes effect with the next base, and development bases can count it and switch it off (`npm run device -- late`) to compare.
