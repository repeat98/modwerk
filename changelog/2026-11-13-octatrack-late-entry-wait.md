Octatrack base (development): the base now measures how late each frame interrupt starts and can hold one that starts 110 to 190 µs late until it is 190 µs late. Octabam (Sam Banks) measured that such a start, right after the long frame at START or a program change, could stop the audio until a power cycle. The wait is off until it is measured on a unit: development bases read the counts and switch it with `npm run device -- late`, `late:on` and `late:off`. It arrives with the next base.
