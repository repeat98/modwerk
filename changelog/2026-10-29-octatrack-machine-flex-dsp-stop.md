Octatrack base (beta): a playing track that leaves Analog BD (or another module machine with its own sound) for FLEX at a bank, pattern or Part change no longer stops the DSP. Each trig on the machine also started stock's hidden FLEX voice and armed its retrig from the machine's settings, and nothing stopped them. When the track became FLEX again, stock rendered them: on a track that had been the machine since power-on, the retrig fired on every sample, and the DSP stopped until a power cycle (#1051). The base now keeps that voice and its retrig stopped while the machine renders the track.
