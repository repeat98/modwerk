Octatrack over USB (beta): while a base update runs, the Octatrack's dot in the sidebar and the status bar and the update dialog's progress line blink red and say not to unplug, also while the unit restarts; before, the dot stayed green as if the unit were idle (a beta tester, 24 October 2026). An Octatrack that another browser window or app is using now connects by itself once it is free (tried every 3 s), and the message says what to close: Modwerk in another browser, Chrome profile or private window, or another app. Limits: the Octatrack's own screen and LEDs still show nothing during the write, and why one tester's computer keeps reporting the unit as in use is not known yet.
