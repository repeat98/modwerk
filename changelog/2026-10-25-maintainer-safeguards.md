Module releases and community: a registered author's automatic release now goes to owner review when it reaches firmware or hardware addresses its published version never used, the owner is told about every automatic release, and followers hear about one 48 hours after it goes live, so a bad or hijacked update can be replaced first. Pull requests with red checks leave the review queue (`needs-work`, closed after 14 quiet days, reopen any time), the pull request template keeps only what no check can prove, and the report forms, forum tips and community rules now say what to expect from module authors and how moderation escalates.
