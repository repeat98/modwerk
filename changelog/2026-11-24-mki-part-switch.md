Fix: the device tools switch Parts and open the Part menu on an Octatrack MKI ([#2099](https://github.com/repeat98/modwerk/issues/2099)). An MKI has no PART key: stock opens its Part window with FUNC + MIDI, where an arrow moves the cursor and YES selects, and FUNC + BANK there opens the Part menu. `part1`-`part4`, `reload` and the new `savepart` step use that on an MKI, so `device:test` gets past its Part switches (test:procedure step 7). `npm run device:emulator -- --mki` boots the emulator as an MKI.
