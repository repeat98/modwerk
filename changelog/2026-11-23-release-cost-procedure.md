Every Octatrack module release now proves its whole cost on a unit before it ships:

- `device:price --curve` also runs an admission test: the module alone on an empty and on a medium pattern must be admitted and play with no dropped render and no stall.
- A release dated from 24 November 2026 is refused without that run, with a curve whose first reading passed 100 % of a frame, with a CPU price measured on another version, or without its worst-case record, whatever day the module was first listed.
