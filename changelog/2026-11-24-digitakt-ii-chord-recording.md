Digitakt II base: chords recorded across linked voices. Live recording on a lead track (SETTINGS > VOICES above 1) now records each note of a chord as a step on the track that plays it, so a recorded chord plays back as a chord and each note can be edited like any step. A note the instrument starts playing a moment before the recording sees it is recorded on the track playing it, not skipped. While linked, the voice tracks take a copy of the lead's sound and of its track's TRIG page defaults, length, swing and speed, kept up to date a part at a time and saved with the project, so their own steps sound exactly like the lead; they keep that sound when the setting returns to 1. Notes from the keys and MIDI fill the group from the lead up whatever the pattern plays there, and the pattern's notes of the lead never land on a voice track with steps of its own. Checked in the emulator and, as reported by the author, on a unit (OS 1.17, Morpha on the lead): chords recorded with the pattern running land on every linked track, sound like the lead and edit like any step.
