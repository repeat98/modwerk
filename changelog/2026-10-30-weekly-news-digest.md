Admin news: **Suggest this week's digest** replaces the monthly suggestion. It drafts the past seven days in a new module-card layout: each new module as a card with its library thumbnail in a two-column grid that stacks on a phone, each update as a row with a small thumbnail and its first release note, then the busiest discussions and showcase posts. News emails can now show pictures from the site's own files, and the site build saves every module's card under a fixed name, `/module-thumbnails/<id>.jpg`, for them. The layout is `modwerk-news-002`; a draft saved earlier takes it when it is next saved. The first weekly digest is in `docs/news/009-weekly-2026-10-30.draft.json`.
