Octatrack base: a module effect that would overload the frame is refused with CPU FULL, and the box after a pick shows the CPU it takes next to its DSP and memory. Until now only the CPU meter judged a pick, which is an average over seconds: on an Octatrack MKII a third Tape Echo was admitted with the meter at about 76 %, then the frame guard dropped about 70 renders a second on the Amiga track (crackling) while the meter read 72 %. The base now keeps the frame's own load and adds the effect's measured price to it. Effects that name no price yet are judged by the meter as before.
