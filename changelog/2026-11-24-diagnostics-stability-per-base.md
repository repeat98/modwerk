The owner's diagnostics summary now has a stability line for each Octatrack base. Each line gives units, starts, logged hours, stalls, crashes and freezes until a restart, the rates per 100 hours and per 100 starts, and how many syncs were kept. OS upgrades and reflashes are left out, and so is the owner's development unit. A unit's brake and late-entry records at a start are counted in one line instead of filling its list of recent records.
