`npm run test:procedure` runs step 5, the emulator rehearsal, by itself when the computer has the adapted `ot_emu` and a development twin (`MODWERK_OT_EMU` and `MODWERK_OT_TWIN`, or `--emulator` and `--twin`). It starts its own emulator session, runs the unit steps' commands there (read the unit, the scripted pass, put back) and stops it, so a fault shows before the unit's turn. Without them the record says which is missing and how to get it; `--no-emulator` leaves the step out.
