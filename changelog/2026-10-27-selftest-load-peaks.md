Octatrack (USB, beta): syncs no longer fail because the CPU or a DSP core went over 90 % during the self-test. A busy pattern reaches that on its own, and while your modules play the Octatrack already refuses an effect that doesn't fit a DSP core and skips a module machine's sound for a moment rather than freezing. When the self-test finds the DSP stopped, it now tells you to switch the Octatrack off and on, instead of blaming a module.
