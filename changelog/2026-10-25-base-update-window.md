Improvement: the Octatrack base update window (USB, beta) is calmer and shows its progress in one place. Before the update it shows the three stages in the unit's order as a row of small cards, one line each, a single note to save the project and keep the Octatrack on until Done, and the recovery steps folded under "If something goes wrong". While the update runs, the same cards become the progress: done stages get a check, the current one is highlighted and carries the send's progress bar, and one status line says what is happening, turning into the "don't switch off" warning only during the save. When an update stops, the card where it stopped is marked and the window says "The update stopped." in plain words, adding "Nothing was saved: your Octatrack keeps its current base" when it stopped before the save. The card-flash warning no longer sits on the USB update; the card download keeps it. Dev previews can show the update with `?preview=base-install&unit=base&base=older&slots=one|two`. Checked with the dialog tests and by the owner in the preview; not checked on a phone.
