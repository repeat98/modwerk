Fix: Octatrack base (USB, beta): too many module voices no longer stall the Octatrack until a power cycle. POLY8, FM Synth and VECTOR render their sound in the frame interrupt, which also keeps USB, the panel and every task waiting while it runs. In a busy project it could take nearly the whole CPU: the panel froze, USB disconnected, the DSP stopped and the sequencer stuck on its first step (#844, #686, #678, a beta tester's freeze with POLY8 and FM Synth). The base now gives the frame interrupt 80 % of each frame. A machine track whose render would overdraw that renders silence for that moment instead: you hear a dropout on that track, and the Octatrack keeps running. Stock tracks and Analog BD always play. The diagnostics log records these dropouts (which tracks, how many). On the owner's MKII, POLY8 0.3.1 on T1, FM Synth on T2 and six stock tracks trigged on every step stalled within half a second of starting USB recording without the guard; with it, the same load and recording ran cleanly, the frame interrupt at 80 %, with POLY8 silent on about half its renders. POLY8 alone, sparse or on every step, was never cut. New beta base; Analog BD, Output Matrix and Sidechain are relinked to it with their code unchanged. Limits: card builds have no base and so no guard (#844 was a card build). The guard cuts the first module track in the frame, not the most expensive one. Checked on development builds of this base, not yet the release image.
