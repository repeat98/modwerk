Octatrack (USB, beta): a synced module set is kept as soon as it is sent. The sync no longer runs the self-test or waits for Keep or Undo, so it can no longer fail and roll back on a busy pattern. The self-test of the installed modules stays: run it from Status on the Octatrack page, or from PROJ > MODWERK > SELF TEST on the unit. The developer MCP's device trial still self-tests and waits for Keep or Undo.
