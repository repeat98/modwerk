Octatrack base: machines that render on the CPU are now budgeted like effects on the DSP. Picking one more than the instrument can carry shows a CPU FULL box instead of dropouts, and a loaded Part that asks for too many keeps the later tracks silent and says so.
 Limits: the prices are first estimates (45 % of the CPU for added machines, 12 % for a machine that names no price) and FM Synth 0.1.2 still plays outside the budget: a pick over the limit is refused, but its voices are not counted or silenced while they play. Seen on development builds on the owner's MKII; the release base itself was not run on a unit.
