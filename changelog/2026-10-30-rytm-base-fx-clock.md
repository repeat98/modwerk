Analog Rytm MKII: the Modwerk base gives send effects the sequencer's time, so delays, gates and filters can follow the pattern's tempo, its speed and its swing, from PLAY. On SEND FX, holding SRC and pressing YES sets A-H at random, and SRC + NO brings the values back once. A kit whose effect is missing now leaves MISSING with FUNC + A: turning right picks the first effect, left the last. The work area each effect borrows is now 32 KB instead of 64 KB, so fifteen send effects fit beside Loop Sync and Retrig Grid; CHORUS, FLANGER and PHASER use 16 KB and play as before. A double press on TRK from the SEND or SEND FX page now shows stock's list cleanly instead of the page drawn over it.
