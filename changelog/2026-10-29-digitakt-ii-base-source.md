Digitakt II: the start of Modwerk's own Digitakt II base. Core 1.1's source is copied unchanged with its provenance, and its additions are proposed for the machine's interface: per-frame parameter values, key LEDs, and per-project and per-kit data that a performance mode needs. Nothing changes for players: the site still builds with core 1.0, and nothing from 1.1 is built or offered yet.
