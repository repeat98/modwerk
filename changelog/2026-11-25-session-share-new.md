Session sharing: `npm run session -- share --new` shares only what came after an earlier share of the same session. Sharing the rest of a long session meant fetching the last share, finding where it ended and listing every earlier entry with `--skip` by hand (seen in a contributor session shared in five parts, 24 November). `--new` reads your own shares made since the session started and leaves out each entry one of them already holds; `preview --new` shows the result first.
