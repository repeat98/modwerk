Diagnostics: when a USB sync stops because the Octatrack did not confirm a transfer, a shared report now also names what stopped it, as one of a few fixed error types: the browser's USB error, no reply in time, a submission the Octatrack dropped, or a reply that did not confirm the step. Until now such a report said only that the transfer failed and at which step, which could not explain syncs that failed every time on a few units. No message text is sent, and the privacy page names the addition.
