Octatrack (USB, beta, with the next base): USB Audio can be switched on the unit, in PROJ > MODWERK > USB AUDIO. Choose ALL CHANNELS, MAIN + CUE ONLY (with the 8 tracks + Main + Cue setup: the track channels stay in your DAW but are silent, and the unit no longer copies them) or OFF, and press YES. OFF and MAIN + CUE ONLY free the processing the unit spends on USB Audio while a computer records; nothing changes when no computer has the stream open, since the unit already sends nothing then. The choice survives a restart. The channels a computer sees still come with the setup chosen on the site (#1512). Not yet measured on a unit.
