Octatrack (USB, beta, with the next base): USB Audio can be switched on the unit, in PROJ > MODWERK > USB AUDIO. Choose ALL CHANNELS, MAIN + CUE ONLY (with the 8 tracks + Main + Cue setup: the track channels stay in your DAW but are silent) or OFF (your DAW gets silence), and press YES. A computer recording all twenty channels costs the unit a lot: on an Octatrack MKII playing a pattern, the audio interrupt took 65 % of the processor with ALL CHANNELS, 52 % with MAIN + CUE ONLY, 44 % with OFF and 39 % with no computer recording. The sound comes back by itself when you choose again, and the choice survives a restart. Nothing changes while no computer has the stream open, since the unit sends nothing then. The channels a computer sees still come with the setup chosen on the site (#1512).
