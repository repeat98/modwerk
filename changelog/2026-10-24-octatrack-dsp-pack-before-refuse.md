Octatrack base (USB, beta): picking an effect on a full DSP core no longer gives "MEMORY FULL" when there is enough free memory in total but it is split into small pieces. The Octatrack now first moves the loaded effects together, then loads the new one; this takes about a third of a second instead of the usual tenth. Before, swapping LO-FI for PLATE REV on a core 89 % full was refused, and the track then played without LO-FI even though the screen still showed it; now a refused pick always gets its old effect back. A pick can still be refused when the effects together don't fit. Beta base `109f23bd`.
