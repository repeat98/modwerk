Octatrack base: a Part's CPU budget charges each machine what one playing track of it was measured to take, when the site's cost table names it, instead of the price its package carries. FM Synth counted 4 % a track and took 16.5 %, so a loaded Part could carry eight of them; now two fit and the rest stay silent with the CPU FULL message. POLY8 is charged 42 % (12 before), UNGEMACH 22 % (15) and VECTOR 1 % (12). A price measured again reaches every unit with the next sync, without a module release.
