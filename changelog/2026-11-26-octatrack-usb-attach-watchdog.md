Octatrack base: when a computer starts connecting to the Octatrack and then gives up part way, for example after a module install, a USB Audio layout change or a restart, the Octatrack now offers itself again within a few seconds, instead of staying off USB until a power cycle. Ships with the next base. Not yet seen healing a dropped connection on a unit; on the owner's MKII the new rule caused no extra reconnects over three restarts, twelve USB Audio installs and two layout changes.
