Developer tools: `npm run upstream:tools` now also keeps the source of Ziddle, Berbasoft's emulator for Zoom ZDL effects, at an archive pinned by its address and SHA-256, and refuses a changed archive or local edits. Build it there with cargo, then check and render your own `.ZDL` files without a pedal or Zoom firmware. Nothing from it enters a Modwerk build, catalogue or release.
