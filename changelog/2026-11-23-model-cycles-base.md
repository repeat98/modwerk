The Model:Cycles gets a Modwerk base in development: drumkilla's core-mc 0.3 from Elekloader v0.11.0, imported unchanged and credited, with room for eight handlers per hook instead of four. Its hooks are listed in `sdk/machines/model-cycles/events.json`, and the machine page shows the core step and the base as started. It boots on a unit and behaves as stock; no module exists yet, and downloads stay off.
