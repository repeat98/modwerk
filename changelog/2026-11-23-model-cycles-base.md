The Model:Cycles gets a Modwerk base in development: drumkilla's core-mc 0.3 from Elekloader v0.11.0, imported unchanged and credited, plus `ev_key_leds`, a hook that lets modules light the key LEDs, as on the Digitakt II. Its six hooks are listed in `sdk/machines/model-cycles/events.json`, and the machine page shows the core step and the base as started. With a test module on the LED hook it runs on a unit. No module exists yet, and downloads stay off.
