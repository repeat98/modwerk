Octatrack base: after installing or removing USB Audio, or changing its layout or inputs, the Octatrack could drop off USB until a power cycle when the computer reset its USB port at the same moment. When the Octatrack has to leave USB in the middle of a transfer, it now stays off for ten seconds so the computer's retries are over before it comes back. Known issue: rarely, after installing or removing a module, the Octatrack still drops off USB until a power cycle (seen through a USB hub on a Mac, about once in 30 installs and removals). Ships with the next base.
