Development: `/modwerk-try-on-unit` is now `/modwerk-test-on-unit`, and it tests in two stages. The agent first runs its own tests on the connected unit (a trial, driving the controls, the loader, crash and load readings, the self-test, screenshots, a dropout check on recorded audio, then Undo). Only a build that passes goes to the developer, with a short list of what a person has to judge: the sound, MIDI, scenes and recording, the other installed modules, and the power cycle. TESTING.md keeps the two apart.
