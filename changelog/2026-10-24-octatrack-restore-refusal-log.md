Octatrack base (USB, beta): when the Octatrack starts and can't put back one of your kept modules, its log now records which module and why (for example "busy" or "in use"). Before, only "refused" was kept, and the reason was lost as soon as the next module installed, so a tester's dropped Ringmod SC couldn't be explained. Shared diagnostics carry it the next time the site reads the log; nothing else changes on the unit. Same beta base `109f23bd`.
