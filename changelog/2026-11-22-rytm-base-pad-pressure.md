Analog Rytm MKII base: Pad Pressure Fix's three fixes join every variant, always on, with no setting. During a retrig roll (RTRG held or ALWAYS ON) a held pad's pressure now also reaches the sound's AFTERTOUCH targets and MIDI poly pressure, as on a plain held pad; with VEL.CUR OFF it still sets the roll's velocity too, which goes past stock (set VEL.CUR for aftertouch alone; a setting for stock's choice may come later). With RTRG held, PERF pads move the performance. CHROMATIC pads send their poly pressure on the note they played, not 24 semitones above. The module leaves the library when this base ships.
