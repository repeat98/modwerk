Octatrack base 0.7.10: a module that listens to other tracks through the key bus (such as SC Ringmod) is no longer turned away with "not enough module memory" when the real reason is another module effect's delay buffer sitting where it reads. The site now says what to do: move the module effect on FX2 of track 1, 5 or 7 to FX2 of another track, then sync again. E-Verb, Mini Verb, BBD Delay and Vibrato on those tracks no longer cause it at all, since the base now knows their buffers stop short of the key bus; Air Chorus there still does, because its buffer really reaches it.
