Beta: when you install an effect on a Zoom pedal and another effect there already has its ID, the pedal page now moves the new one to a free ID from 958 to 1023 instead of refusing it, and says so. Nothing in the effect changes but its ID. Installing an update of a moved effect keeps its new ID, so your patches still find it. Added effects can now use IDs up to 1023; an MS-70CDR ran, saved and recalled effects at 917, 1000 and 1023.
