Octatrack: `scripts/octatrack-modport.py` turns a ProTracker module (.mod, 4, 6 or 8 channels) into an Octatrack project, written as a copy of an empty project the unit made. Each channel becomes a Flex track, each song position a pattern with the module's notes as trigs and sample, pitch, volume and sample-offset locks, each sample a WAV in the project's own folder, and the tempo comes from the song's speed and BPM. `--report` lists what would be converted and what is left out (slides, vibrato, arpeggio and other effects) without writing anything.
