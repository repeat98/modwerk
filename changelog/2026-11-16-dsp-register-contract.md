Development: `module:doctor` has a `dsp registers` line. `scripts/dsp-register-contract.py` reads the assembled words of every Octatrack DSP package entry (effect init and proc, hook engines) and reports the registers each writes against its contract: a hook site's `clobbers` in `dsp-sites.json`, and an init keeps r1, n1 and m1. It reports only for now; the owner sets the date from which it refuses.
