Docs: a mod for elekloader's shop and a change to an elekloader core now point to elekloader's repository, its Submit a mod form and its core contributing guide. The Octatrack, Analog Rytm MKII and Digitakt II bases are described as Modwerk's own, not elekloader cores. The Rytm base guide notes that it stays on core-ar2 0.7 and that core-ar2 0.8 and later can misread its FX-track locks in a project moved between the two.
