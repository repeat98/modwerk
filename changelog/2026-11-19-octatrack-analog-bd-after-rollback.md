Fix: Octatrack base 0.7.10 plays Analog BD again after a trial that took it out and was rolled back. A trial that removed Analog BD, Sidechain or Output Matrix (to make room for another module, say) let the base let go of the module's engine for good while the trial was still running, so after the rollback the module was listed and could be picked but made no sound until a power cycle. The base now keeps the module ready to come back until the trial is kept or rolled back, while still giving its DSP room to the trial. Reproduced and fixed on an MKII running a development copy of the beta base, with the same `set` trial the report used, and in host tests for both the single-module and the whole-set trial.
