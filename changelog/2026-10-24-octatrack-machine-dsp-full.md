Octatrack base (beta): a machine that doesn't fit its DSP core now says so. Before, putting Analog BD on a track whose DSP chip was already full (T1-T4 and T5-T8 are separate chips) left the track silent with no message. Now the DSP FULL box appears, reading "T1 SRC" and "ANALOG BD NEEDS +20%" with the chip's DSP and memory bars, for three seconds or until a key. Free some effects on that chip and put the machine on again. Checked on a development build on an MKII with Analog BD on a bank whose T1-T4 chip was full; not yet on the release image.
