Octatrack base 0.8: when a computer never picks the Octatrack up on USB after it starts (seen after RAM boots, about one in three on one unit), the base now notices within five seconds, drops off the bus for half a second and offers itself again, as unplugging and replugging the cable would; it tries up to five times and records each attempt in the unit's diagnostics log as a `usb` incident, so a repeat shows which side stalled. Issue #1985.
