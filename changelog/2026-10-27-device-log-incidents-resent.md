Fix: incidents from the Octatrack's own log are no longer skipped when their upload fails. The base logs load records and incidents (freezes, crashes, DSP stalls, overloads, starts) while no site is open, and the site sends them at the next connection. Loads and incidents share one record number, but the browser kept one mark for both: the load groups went first and moved it past the incidents, so an incident group that failed, or was still waiting when the tab closed, was never sent again. Each kind now has its own mark, which moves only past groups stored in order; a failed group goes again at the next read, ten minutes later or at the next connection, and the groups after it are duplicates the server ignores. The mark kept before this change is where both start. Checked with collector tests (the new one fails on the old code). Not changed: the unit holds 96 load records (about eight hours of running) and 32 incidents (each power-up takes two or three), so a longer time without a connection still overwrites the oldest; a larger log needs a base release. Not yet seen on the live site.
