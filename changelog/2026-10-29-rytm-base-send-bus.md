Analog Rytm MKII: the Modwerk base now carries the send bus. Every track's send, the SEND and SEND FX pages, the effect chooser, LEV, the CPU reading and the FX track's p-locks belong to the base, and each send effect is its own module that joins the base's effect list. With no effect installed the base keeps the stock pages. Nothing changes for players yet: the site still builds with irpina's core-ar2 0.7 until it moves to this base, and CHORUS, FLANGER and PHASER follow as their own modules.
