Digitakt II base: linked voices. SETTINGS gets a VOICES row for the track you are on (1 to 8). With more than 1, that track leads the tracks after it: each new note it plays, from its trigs, the keys or MIDI, goes to the next free track of the group, so long notes ring on while new ones start and live or MIDI chords sound on several tracks at once. The voice tracks play the lead's sound with each trig's own locks and follow its knob turns; their own sounds and trigs stay as they were. A muted lead stays silent, and a muted voice track is skipped. The setting is saved with the project. Every machine plays this way, the stock ones and the ones modules add. With every track at 1 nothing changes. Checked in the emulator only, not yet on a unit.
