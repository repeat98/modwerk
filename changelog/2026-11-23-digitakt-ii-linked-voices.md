Digitakt II base: linked voices. SETTINGS gets a VOICES row for the track you are on (1 to 8). With more than 1, that track leads the tracks after it: each new note it plays, from its trigs, the keys or MIDI, goes to the first free track of the group from the lead on, so long notes ring on while new ones start, live or MIDI chords sound on several tracks at once, and the lead plays a chord's first note. While linked, the voice tracks belong to the lead: they play its sound and machine with each trig's own locks and follow its knob turns (a linked track that is not playing is kept an exact copy of the lead), and their own trigs are silent; their own sounds and trigs come back as they were when the setting returns to 1. A muted lead stays silent, and a muted voice track is skipped. The setting is saved with the project. Every machine plays this way, the stock ones and the ones modules add. Added machines also get each track's knobs C and E at full resolution on the sound chip (knob C used to arrive only as off or on). With every track at 1 nothing changes. Tested on a Digitakt II with an added machine (Morpha) as the lead: chords from the keys, knobs turned while playing, saving and reloading the project; MIDI chords, sequencer trigs on a lead and a stock machine as the lead are not yet tested on a unit.
