Octatrack base 0.8, the Part Edit window (FUNC+PART): a module that is inactive can leave its row out, and a module that takes over what PART's rows act on can leave PART out while it does. With PART left out and one module's row left, FUNC+PART opens stock's popup with that module's actions in PART's place. PART comes back as soon as the last module leaving it out is removed, even with other rows left. That popup draws its actions' names, and when the last module's row goes while the window is open, the window is stock's again and its keys work. Nothing changes until a module asks: Kits is the first planned to.
