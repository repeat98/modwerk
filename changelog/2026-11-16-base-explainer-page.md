Site: a new page, Base firmware (`#base`, marked New under Community & help in the sidebar and the phone menu), explains what the base is. As on Help & FAQ, it shows one machine at a time, starting with the one you have selected: Octatrack, MS-70CDR, Analog Rytm, or the other instruments and where each stands. The Octatrack view shows what the base adds on the unit's own display (the startup, PROJ › MODWERK, the CUE + PROJ status, the self-test, the shared machine and effect lists and the base update screen), how it is installed, updated and removed, and what keeps it safe. The Octatrack pictures are emulator captures of the release base and a development twin of it.
