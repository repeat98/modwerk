Development: Digitakt, Digitone and Rytm module builds accept `-fno-tree-loop-distribute-patterns`, which keeps GCC from turning a loop into a `memset` or `memcpy` call a mod cannot link; the warnings `-Wmissing-prototypes` and `-Wstrict-prototypes`; and `-Wno-error=incompatible-pointer-types`, for source written before GCC 14 made an `int` and `int32_t` function-pointer mismatch an error. fxkit, MUTINY and irpina's effect pedals compile with them. Modwerk's Digitone build also knows the DDR area where an effect pedal keeps its large buffers (0x44000000-0x47BE0000), as the elekloader kit's profile does.
