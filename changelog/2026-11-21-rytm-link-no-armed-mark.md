SDK: an Analog Rytm MKII development base no longer draws the USB link's armed mark, a 2 x 5 black notch at the right end of the header bar that ran into stock's stop square and looked like a drawing fault. The header is stock's again. Arming is unchanged: the link is armed at every boot, GLOBAL > REMOTE CTRL shows it, and `rytm_link.py ident` reports it. The test runner's screen compares skip those 10 pixels, so references recorded with the mark still pass. Release bases never carried the link and do not change.
