Site: with a Digitakt, Syntakt or other Elektron device plugged in beside the Octatrack, the site connects to the Octatrack. Before, it could open whichever device Chrome listed first and say the Octatrack runs the original OS, and plugging in another Elektron device could take the link away from the unit. Chrome's device list now shows Octatracks only, and a remote debug session picks the Octatrack the same way.
