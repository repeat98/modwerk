Unit queue: a session can keep the unit between its runs, and the owner can put a release first in line. `npm run unit -- run --keep 10m` (or `MODWERK_UNIT_KEEP=10m` for device:test, test:procedure and the device commands) keeps the turn for the same checkout after its command ends, so a rerun or the screenshots go ahead and no other session takes the unit in the gap. `--priority "why"` puts a command first in line, tells everyone it overtakes why, and asks whoever has the unit to give it back at a safe point; nothing is taken from a running command. On 25 November 2026 a release lost the unit twice in the seconds between two of its runs.
