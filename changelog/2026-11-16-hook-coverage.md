Octatrack SDK: a check that every hook and table in `events.json` has a user (a module, draft, module adapter, the base's build or a test that subscribes, raises or contributes to it) or a one-line reason in `sdk/machines/octatrack/unused-hooks.json`; a reason whose hook is now used, or gone, fails too. It starts with 15 hooks that wait for their first module: the project, bank and Part hooks and `ev_modules_ready` from 14 November, and the MIDI generator table.
