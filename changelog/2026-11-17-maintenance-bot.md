Worker and CI: a maintenance bot reviews pull requests and issues every 30 minutes and acts within a fixed policy. It labels and asks reporters for details at once; it merges green pull requests, closes issues and replies after a waiting window the owner can stop from Telegram; and it asks before anything sensitive. A report arrives each morning. The Worker keeps its ledger (migration 0091) and answers Telegram's buttons. It stays off until the owner runs `npm run maintenance-bot:setup` ([docs/MAINTENANCE_BOT.md](../docs/MAINTENANCE_BOT.md)).
