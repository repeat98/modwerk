Site and SDK: the Analog Rytm MKII base's voices variant (dd771eae, was e241e031) leaves out the FX track's lock words (`locks.c`, `locks.s` and their three stock sites). Only the send bus writes those words, and voices has no send bus, so they did nothing there. A set with a digital voice machine and no send effect, such as Analog BD, gets 1,752 B more RAM for its modules. On voices a pattern's send-FX locks are not kept: one saved with them on send-bus plays without them, and the next save of its locks there frees them.
