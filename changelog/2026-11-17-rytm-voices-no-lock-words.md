Site and SDK: the Analog Rytm MKII base's voices variant (dd771eae, was e241e031) leaves out the FX track's lock words (`locks.c`, `locks.s` and their three stock sites). Only the send bus writes those words, and voices has no send bus, so they did nothing there. A set with a digital voice machine and no send effect, such as Analog BD, gets 1,752 B more RAM for its modules. On voices a pattern's send-FX locks are not kept: one saved with them on send-bus plays without them there. Once it is saved on voices, its send-FX locks are lost: back on send-bus, the steps that had them play wrong A-H values until those locks are cleared or set again, as after a set without the send bus (plain).
