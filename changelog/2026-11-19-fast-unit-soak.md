Development: `scripts/octatrack-soak.mjs` plays a seeded run of USB MIDI at an Octatrack (program changes, START and STOP, scenes, the crossfader, mutes and FX knobs, about 20 a second), reads the new `health` step after every operation, stops at the first crash, stall, loader error or freeze with the unit's records in its log, replays a run, and shrinks a failure to its shortest prefix. `scripts/octatrack-midi-bridge.py` sends the MIDI from the computer the unit is plugged into.
