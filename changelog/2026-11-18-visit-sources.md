Site: the site statistics now show where visits come from (search engines, AI assistants, Reddit, YouTube, Discord, press sites, email or direct) and how the sign-in prompt is answered before a build and elsewhere, so we can see which channels bring people and where sign-ups stop. Your browser turns the referring site or a link's campaign tag into one category from a fixed list; the address itself is never sent, nothing is stored on your device, and Do Not Track, Global Privacy Control or an objection on the privacy page stop it. The privacy page names both counts.

Links to the site in Modwerk's own emails (news, activity, welcome and beta mail) now say which kind of email they came from, so the statistics can tell which mails bring people back. The tag names only the kind of email, nothing about you, and the site removes it from the address bar once read. Password and verification emails are not tagged. Links to the site in TARS's replies on Discord are tagged the same way, so the statistics show what the bot's answers bring. Push notifications, the forum feed and the Share button on a discussion tag their links too.
