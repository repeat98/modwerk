Octatrack base: a new hook, `ev_engine_job`, at the engine task's job switch (`0x4008485e`). A module that has to act before the engine runs a job, or that runs job types of its own, subscribes to it instead of patching the site, which the core logger already uses on every build. The logger still records each job first, and the sample editor's job seam is unchanged. On card builds, which have no base, a module keeps its own detour there, and the logger now hands the site on to it rather than refusing the build. Nothing subscribes yet; FM Synth 0.1.4 is the first module that needs it.
