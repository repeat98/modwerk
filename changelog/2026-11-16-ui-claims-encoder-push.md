Improvement: Octatrack modules can claim pressing an encoder (`PUSHA`-`PUSHF`) in `compatibility.uiClaims`, apart from turning it, and up to 24 claims instead of 16. A module that acts on a pressed knob (`key:TRIG+PUSHD`: a trig held, knob D pressed) could only claim the turn, so the gate flagged a clash with every module that uses a trig held and that knob turned, such as Arp Key Locks on ARP SETUP. The cap left no room for one press under TRIG, SCENE A and SCENE B (18 claims). Checked with the contract and gate tests (both fail without the change).
