Fix: Octatrack base update over USB (beta) now gets through a USB dropout during the save. The Octatrack writes the new base on its own and finishes even if the cable drops. But the page then showed "The Octatrack stopped answering" with a Try again button, which invited switching the unit off at the one moment that can leave it unable to start without the startup menu's MIDI recovery. Now the page says "Keep the Octatrack switched on", waits for the unit to come back and saves again until the unit has restarted on the new base from its flash memory. Saving again is harmless: the base compares its flash first and writes nothing when it already holds the new base. A save that failed its own check is also repeated now, instead of stopping with an error. A refusal before anything was written still stops at once, as before. If the save is still not confirmed after four tries, the page asks to keep the unit on and try again. The page also asks before it is closed during an update, and says the save takes under a minute. Checked with link tests on the simulated unit: a dropout mid-save, a failed check, and a save that never confirms (all three fail without this change). Not checked on a unit or in a browser. A power cut during the save still needs the startup menu's MIDI recovery; a two-slot base that makes it harmless is in the works.
