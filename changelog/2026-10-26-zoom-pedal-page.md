Beta: a Zoom MultiStomp gets its pedal page, the Octatrack page for the pedal (`#zoom-ms-70cdr/pedal`, beta testers only). Over Web MIDI in Chrome or Edge it shows the pedal's model and firmware, lists what's on it and backs up every file into a zip; a backup has every file or saves nothing. It writes nothing to the pedal. Visitors without the beta who open the address go to the machine's library. Its Setup page shows the two routes, as on the Octatrack: effect files on Zoom's firmware, and MIDISTOMP as the base underneath, to be built and flashed through Modwerk later. The Zoom module contract's ID ledger records GRDLOOP (497).
