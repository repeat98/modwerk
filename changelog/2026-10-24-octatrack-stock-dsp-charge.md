Octatrack base (USB, beta): more module effects fit before DSP FULL. Each effect slot used to book DSP time as if it ran the most expensive stock effect (DJ EQ), even on FILTER, DELAY or NONE. Stock effects alone booked 77 to 88 % of a DSP core, and one or two module reverbs filled it while the meter read about 45 %. Each slot now books the most expensive stock effect this bank's Parts use in that slot (FILTER 285 cycles, DELAY nothing). In a bank of FILTER and DELAY, three Mini Verbs now fit on tracks 1-4 and two on tracks 5-8, where two and one fit before; the self-test of that set peaked at 74 % and 69 %. A Part switch or a RELOAD is never refused for a stock effect. The one new refusal: a stock effect more expensive than anything the bank uses in that slot shows DSP FULL when the module effects in this or another Part of the bank leave no room for it. The STATUS page's DSP rows and the DSP FULL box show the bank's fullest Part. New beta base `049efc39`; Analog BD, Output Matrix and Sidechain are relinked to it with their code unchanged. Limits: Analog BD voices and Sidechain's engine still book each slot at the old price, because a bank change keeps them. Checked on development builds of this base on the owner's MKII, not yet the release image.
