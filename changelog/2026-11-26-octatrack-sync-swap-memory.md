Octatrack: a sync that takes modules out and puts others in is now priced as the Octatrack runs it. The Octatrack keeps the modules a sync takes out until the new ones are in, so a set that fits afterwards could still be refused for module memory after the transfer. The site now stops such a sync before anything is sent and says what to do: "First sync with Mini Verb and LOFI2 taken out and Midiverb I/II left out, then add Midiverb I/II and sync again." When the Octatrack itself refuses a module for memory, the site now says that modules taken out since it started can leave gaps, and to restart the Octatrack and sync again.
