Octatrack over USB: a sync no longer says “Kept, but the Octatrack couldn’t save … to the card” when the Octatrack simply had not saved yet. After a large sync the unit can be busy for up to a minute before it writes the set to the card; the site waited 20 seconds and then reported a failed save, although the set was saved shortly after. It now says the modules are synced and will be saved once the Octatrack is stopped and idle, and to leave it on until Status shows “Saved and checked”. A save the Octatrack itself reports as failed still says so.
 Limits: found in shared diagnostics (four syncs on three units); not reproduced on a unit, and why those units stayed busy that long is not known yet.
