Your Octatrack: after a failed sync, connection, self-test or base update, **Copy details** and **Save report** next to the error give you what went wrong, the base with its USB Audio layout and the last USB events, to paste in a message or keep as a file. The page keeps its last 100 USB events in memory whether or not you share diagnostics, and sends none of them by itself. **Report a problem** now fills in what the Octatrack runs and the last error, and offers to attach those events; you see what is attached and can remove it before posting. A failed base update that changes only the USB Audio layout now names the layout ("came back on base 0.8.1 (USB Audio off)") instead of reading like the same base.
