Development: every place a module attaches to the firmware is now called a hook: the base's shared hooks on the Octatrack, its DSP hooks, a module's own hooks and the core hooks on the elemod machines. The guides, AGENTS.md, the new-module skill, the machine READMEs, the compatibility gate's messages and the site's platform text say "hook" where they said "event", and `sdk/machines/octatrack/EVENTS.md` is now `HOOKS.md`. The guide sections are now [Hooks](docs/module-guides/README.md#hooks) and [Propose a hook](docs/module-guides/README.md#propose-a-hook). The registry's file names (`events.json`, `events.py`, `modwerk_events.h`, `runtime_events.*`) and `runtime.json`'s `event` key keep the old word until the next base build: the base loads them by those names, so renaming them changes the base. Nothing on the unit changes.
