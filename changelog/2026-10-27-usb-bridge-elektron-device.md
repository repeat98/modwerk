Octatrack USB bridge (`scripts/octatrack-usb-bridge.py`): with another Elektron device on the computer's USB, such as an Analog Rytm on Overbridge, the bridge took the first Elektron device it found and every request to the Octatrack failed. It now opens the device that offers the Modwerk interface. Checked in `sdk/tests/test_usb_bridge.py` and on a MKII with a Rytm MKII attached. The Octatrack driving guide adds the trap.
