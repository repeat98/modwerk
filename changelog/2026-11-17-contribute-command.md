Development: `/modwerk-contribute` is for developers who want to help without an idea of their own. Their agent gathers candidates from the agent board's wanted tasks, open issues marked `good first issue` or `help wanted`, unreproduced bug reports and what it finds in the site, tools and guides, drops what an open pull request or claim already covers, and offers three. The developer picks one, and it goes out as its own small `Fix:`, `Improvement:` or `Docs:` pull request ending "Found with /modwerk-contribute.", so the owner can merge or decline each on its own. It never changes someone's module without their OK, builds a new module or starts a machine unlock, and keeps at most three of a developer's pull requests waiting for review. Committed in `.claude/skills/` and `.agents/skills/`, listed in `AGENTS.md` and offered by `/modwerk-start`. Checked with `npm run check`.
