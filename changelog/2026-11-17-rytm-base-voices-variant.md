Site and SDK: the Analog Rytm MKII base gains a third variant, voices (e241e031): send-bus's digital voices and FX-track lock words without the send bus, its pages, its clock or the FX list. The site now builds a set with a digital voice machine and no send effect, such as Analog BD, on voices, which leaves about 17.7 KB more RAM for modules than send-bus. A set's storage lockfile carries over between the two: the send bus's settings keep their bits while it is away and return with their values. `machine:base` takes a variant's `leave` patterns, and `modules:check` refuses a voice machine that uses the send bus's code.
