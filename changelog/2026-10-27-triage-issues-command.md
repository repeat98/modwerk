Development: `/modwerk-triage-issues` goes through all open issues in one pass, where `/modwerk-fix-issue` takes one. It reads and sorts each issue by the issue worker's rules in `docs/MAINTENANCE.md`, replies where a reporter must answer or a fix already exists, then gives the invoker a list of closures with reasons and a work plan for the confirmed bugs and features: in order, grouped by shared cause, with who does each. It closes and starts work only on what the invoker picks. Committed in `.claude/skills/` and `.agents/skills/` and listed in `AGENTS.md`. Checked with `npm run check`.
