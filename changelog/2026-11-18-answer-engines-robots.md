Site: AI search and answer tools such as ChatGPT search, Claude and Perplexity may now read modwerk.app, so someone asking an assistant how to mod their instrument can be pointed to the right module page. Crawlers that collect pages for AI training stay turned away. Until now the site's build replaced the crawler policy in `public/robots.txt` with a plain "allow everyone", so none of its rules reached modwerk.app. The build now publishes that policy with the sitemap line added.
