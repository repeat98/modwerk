Improvement: Octatrack base (USB, beta) and SDK for Sidechain Compressor 0.2.0.
- A module's DSP section can carry a second table, `ptable2`, placed straight after its `ptable` and loaded by its own `$fab2e0` literal, as upstream octabam's builder places it. This is supported in the vendored octabam schema and builder, the resident DSP engine and the hooked DSP packages. A `$fab2e0` literal left unfilled is refused. Sidechain Compressor needs it: 0.2.0's first build took the key filters' table address from a register the gain store had already moved, which gave silence, level jumps and squeals on KBAS and KWID (the author's MKI, MW_SC020).
- An in-place effect page (the MWPP record) may name up to 16 of the module's own routines, up from 8. Sidechain's page names 9: MON's formatter, switch and step handler, KEY's formatter, switch and step handler, KBAS's and KWID's formatters and KWID's clip.
- The power-up check hides slots 7 to 11 of a side-chain COMPRESSOR from stock's validator, which judges them against stock's value counts. 0.2.0 stores MON as 2 (OFF) or 3 (ON), and stock's count of 2 would refuse the whole bank.
- The key bus tap is one pass: 25 executed instructions a sample for a core's four tracks, down from 41. Its charge stays at the 51 cycles measured on the old tap until the new one is measured.
Checked: the resident-engine, controller, board, power-up and Sidechain tests; native and browser images of every Sidechain selection match (`module:verify`); no pair newly refused.
