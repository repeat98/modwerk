Developer docs: the Analog Rytm guide adds what Audio In FX found on OS 1.74. It covers where performance locks land in the engine words, the converters' names, and how to feed the delay and reverb sends. It covers which FX words are free and saved, and how a stock page takes a mod's knobs. It also notes that scene and performance mode use different views on the FX track than on the pad tracks, and that stock's key table can report a pad as held when it isn't.
