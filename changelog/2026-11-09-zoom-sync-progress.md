Zoom pedal page: a sync shows the step it is on (which effect, n of m, and how far its write is) and asks you not to unplug the pedal, and the set view shows the pedal's free space and what the sync needs. A set that doesn't fit no longer starts a sync. A failed step stops the sync, says which step it was and that the ones before it are done, and offers the effect list from before the sync; Sync is off wherever Install and Remove are.
