Rytm base: both base variants own the amp envelope's record stores, so modules share them instead of patching them. A module can hold a track's voice silent (`mw_amp_silence`) while its envelope runs on, or subscribe an entry to `ev_amp_record` to rewrite or follow each voice's record in a fixed order. With nothing subscribed the audio is unchanged. The base packages are rebuilt and the Rytm guide has the section.
