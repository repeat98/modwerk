Octatrack base (USB, beta): MIDI generators on the MIDI tracks. A new base table, `ot_midi_generators`, lists every installed generator in MIDI NOTE SETUP's empty left half (double-tap SRC, or double-tap the track key in MIDI mode, which stock ignores): UP, DOWN and YES choose, and CHAN, BANK, PROG and SBNK stay as they were. A generator names the MIDI pages it draws on; as the owner chose on 24 October 2026, generators take SRC and AMP (FUNC+AMP: a second AMP page), while LFO, FX1 and FX2 stay the track's own, FX1 and FX2 kept for MIDI effects later. Holding a trig or a scene shows stock's page, so locks stay stock's. The base keeps the choice in the track's arp step mask and keeps the stock arpeggiator off there; the ARP page is always the generator's, because its settings live in the arp note bytes. With no generator installed every one of these gestures does what stock does. Card builds get the same through the generator module itself. On the owner's MKII the base's part costs 2 µs a UI tick with no generator and up to 12 µs on a generator's page while playing. The beta base becomes `52b22249…` (its USB Audio variants with it); Recorder Loop Fix's base-bound package is relinked, the other base-bound packages only follow the new base. No module uses it yet; VECTOR's MIDI generator follows in its own pull request. Also: `build_core.py --table-owners` works without `--resident-stock-hooks` (it could not import `machine_layout`), the development profile has four slots for the MIDI generators and documents the fx map check's two, and the device session can press PAGE.
