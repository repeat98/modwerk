Zoom pedals (beta): installing and removing effects is safer. When writing the effect list fails while removing an effect, the page writes the original back and still offers the list from before for saving. A failed update of an installed effect is tried once more; if it fails again, the effect comes out of the list and off the pedal, and the page asks you to install it again before power-cycling. Effects are only written to an MS-70CDR on Zoom's firmware 2.10 or the base: on any other pedal or firmware, Install and Remove are off.
