Your Octatrack: failed connections and syncs now say what to do next.

- A sync refused because the last change is still being saved to the card says so, and to press STOP and wait for “Saved and checked”.
- When a sync stops while sending twice in a row, the dialog offers the same set as the card file.
- Closing the device picker without choosing an Octatrack opens “Can’t connect?”, which now covers an Octatrack missing from the list.
- “Isn’t answering” appears only after ten seconds; most connections arrive by themselves before that.
