The developer MCP loads modules onto a Model:Cycles running a Modwerk base over its link: `device_review`, `device_trial`, `device_keep` and `device_undo`, one change a trial, and `device_status` lists the live modules. A Keep or Undo the unit refuses (while a pattern plays) leaves the trial on to try again, and `device_status` says when a trial was lost, or kept by a Keep whose answer went missing.
